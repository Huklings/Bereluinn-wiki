# Former Countries of the Levont

Five sketched countries that joined to form the [[Levont]]. None is settled (2.2).

| 5 | **Reikmark** (named 2 Nov), the old seat | Prestige, law, archives. **The country that brokered the union.** Its old seat is now the capital. An old human kingdom; the land around the capital is still called Reikmark, and its people are **Reikmarkers** |

**The old border kingdom** (answered, 7.1): once rich and powerful, purely human, and the origin of the [[Order of the Rising Sun]]. It is older than the Sundering: [[The Sunturner]] was one of its people before she ascended, and its knights followed her into the war (decided 22 Oct). Its wealth is now held by the Order rather than the region. **Where (22 Oct):** the Levont's western frontier, along [[The March]]. The Order now holds the northern marches but not the main gate (decided 22 Oct); the suggestion is that the southern stretch, gate and all, went to union nobles at the founding.

## After the founding
The old countries were redrawn into six counties that cut across their borders ([[Counties of the Levont]]). They survive as grudges, family ties and dialects, not on the map.

## Working assumption
Species cut across borders. Every old country has a majority plus at least two significant minorities (2.3).
