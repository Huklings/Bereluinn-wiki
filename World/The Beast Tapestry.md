# The Beast Tapestry

One of [[The Tapestries]], connected to beasts. It's Beredluinn's own version of a feywild.

**Its scope (decided 26 Oct):** not only beasts, but the wilds, nature, change, chaos and so on. Each tapestry is permeated by its lord's power as it was when it was made ([[The Tapestries#How they were made (decided 26 Oct)]]).

**Its owner (decided 31 Oct): [[Tapio]].** His clergy worship him but keep his tapestry from spilling over, keeping the balance; the [[Green Wardens]] hunt what comes through.

## What we know
- **Who lives there:** two-legged woodland animals, a little like beastmen but in the spirit of Redwall or Bloomburrow.
- **Size:** the talking animals are roughly dwarf-sized, much larger than their real counterparts.
- **Dire beasts:** the animals that don't talk are larger too, and mutated, like dire bears and dire wolves.
- **Spillover:** dire beasts sometimes turn up in the mortal realm close to this tapestry.

## A known crossing (25 Oct)
The fog of the **Calenwood** in [[Merelon]] is a thin spot onto this tapestry. People who wander deep into it sometimes cross into the beastfolk's realm. A druid circle of [[Tapio]]'s faith guards the wood.

## To the Levont, a fairy tale (decided 25 Oct)
To almost everyone in the Levont, the talking beastfolk and their societies are fairy tales and bedtime stories. Nobody thinks they're real, so nobody gives them a voice or a thought. The few who know better, such as the druids of the [[Calenwood Circle]], keep quiet.

## Who left, and who guards it (26 Oct)
- **The [[Toad People]] came from here.** The other beastfolk cast them out into the mortal realm for hunger, heresy, dark dealings, foul alchemy and mutating others. Hunger is the root.
- **The beast giants** have strong ties to this tapestry: sharper teeth, horns, brass and leather armour, shamanistic spells. They hate the toads too ([[Giants]]).
- *Suggestion:* the dire beasts may be the toads' old alchemy, still breeding after the exile.
- *Suggestion:* a rule for the realm, "everything that stays grows larger", would explain dwarf-sized beastfolk and dire beasts in one line. The toads' hunger made them grow faster than anything should.

## Reading it through existing lore (suggestions, not canon)
- **Why they're big:** time inside a tapestry mutates any being ([[The Tapestries]]). The talking animals may be ordinary animals changed over generations.
- **How they get out:** a realm touches the world only where [[The Veil]] grows thin. The dire beasts would come through thin places.
- **Who hunts them:** the [[Green Wardens]] hunt anything mutated before it wrecks an ecosystem. Dire beasts from this tapestry would be their everyday work.
- **Whose tapestry is it?** (Answered 31 Oct: [[Tapio]]'s.) The "primal tapestries" in the Area 5 lore ([[areas of the Levont]]) could include this one.
- **The Beast Lord:** Area 5's valley has a self-proclaimed Beast Lord. Is he tied to this tapestry?
