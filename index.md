title: Bereluinn

Bereluinn is a world still living in the shadow of the war that broke it. About six hundred years ago [[The Sundering]] ended [[The Elden Era]]: wizard-lords made war on each other, a moon shattered, and the victors ascended as gods ([[The Apotheosis]]). In [[The Current Era]] magic is weaker than it once was, [[Moon Shards]] still fall from the sky and power [[The Railroad]], and the gods can reach the world only through their clerics and the thin places in [[The Veil]].

## Lands

- **[[Levont]]**: a union of six counties under a council, with its capital at Neuhof. See also [[Counties of the Levont]], [[Government of the Levont]] and [[Great Houses of the Levont]].
- **[[Lorweir]]**: steppe bordering the Levont, ruled by a hobgoblin horde.
- **[[Northern Continent]]**: the still-unnamed home of the [[Northern Elves]].
