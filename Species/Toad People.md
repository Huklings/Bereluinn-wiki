# Toad People

A foul species from the island continent south of the Levont that clashes with Levont forces again and again. "Toad people" is a working name.

## What we know (decided 26 Oct)
- **Of the tapestries, but exiled.** They come from [[The Beast Tapestry]], and the other beastfolk cast them out into the mortal realm.
- **Why they were cast out:** hunger, heresy, dark dealings, foul alchemy, and corruption such as mutating others.
- **Hunger is the root sin.** The rest grew out of it: hunger drove them to alchemy, alchemy needed the dark dealings, and mutation was the result.
- **An endless appetite, not a famine.** Nothing ever filled them. That keeps them a threat that is still growing, not a tragic people.
- **A threat like the orcs, from another direction.** The horde presses on the Levont from [[Lorweir]] in the west, and the toads come from the south.
- **Home:** the small island continent south of the Levont. They are probably not alone there.
- **To the Levont, they are simply a foul species** that often clashes with Levont forces. The exile, the hunger and the alchemy are truths for players to uncover later.
- **The beast giants hate them too** ([[Giants]]).

## Why they matter to the Levont
- **A second front.** Every defence the Levont has faces inland, toward [[The March]] and the passes. The toads strike where none of it looks.
- **The fleet is far away.** The standing fleet's port is planned north of the capital, so a raid on the south coast gets a long head start ([[Navies of the Levont]]).
- **Two fronts at once would break the Levont.** It can just about answer one attack at a time ([[World Scale]]). The horde and the toads moving together is the nightmare.

## Suggestions, not canon
- **The dire beasts may be their old experiments.** The beast tapestry's mutated, oversized beasts could be what toad alchemy left behind, still breeding long after the exile. That would give the beastfolk a concrete reason to cast them out.
- **Tears in the weft may be their work** ([[The Veil#Words for crossings (26 Oct)]]). Forced crossings would fit both their alchemy and how they reached the mortal realm.
- **An irony for players to find.** The Levont thinks the beastfolk are bedtime stories ([[The Beast Tapestry#To the Levont, a fairy tale (decided 25 Oct)]]), yet it fights the toads every year. It has no idea the "swamp-things from the south" came out of the fairy-tale realm.
- **Their own story.** The beastfolk remember one great crime. The toads may tell it as survival. A version of events they believe in makes them far more interesting to meet at the table.
