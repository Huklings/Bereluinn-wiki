# The Martyr

The goddess who chose to die. She was an **elven wizard-lord whom the people of the Elden Era already called their moon goddess**, and she was making her mantle when she died. During [[The Sundering]], the Martyr realised she had to die and let her unfinished mantle shatter, so that [[The Tapestries]] could be walled off from the mortal realm. Her death was the Moonshard Calamity ([[The Moon's Shattering]]): a moon broke, and a god died. She is still worshipped as the goddess of sacrifice.

## What we know
- **Who she was:** an elven wizard-lord. Like the other lords, ordinary people already called her a god, and to them she was the moon goddess ([[Pantheon#Where the gods came from (decided 26 Oct)]]).
- **Her mantle never finished (26 Oct).** She was creating her mantle and died before it fully materialised. That is what broke the moon, the only moon that has ever broken, and made the shards.
- **Her tapestry is broken (26 Oct).** It exists but doesn't fully work. She is broken and gone. It still works in part: her unfinished mantle may still hold some magic, and some powers come through to her clerics.
- **Moon nights (your idea, 26 Oct):** on nights when the largest piece of the broken moon is visible, the veil thins heavily across the lands ([[The Veil]]).
- **Her death:** a deliberate sacrifice, before her ascension was complete. That is why her power shattered raw, before it ever became a finished essence ([[Moon Shards]]). Her shattering blocked the tapestry realms off from the mortal realm. The tapestries themselves were made by the mantle ritual ([[The Tapestries#How they were made (decided 26 Oct)]]).
- **Her myth:** the gods fought among themselves, and the weakest bound the others to their domains. In light of her death, "bound to their domains" means sealed into their tapestries.
- **Domains:** Moon, Sorrow, Duty, Protection (31 Oct, below).
- **Form:** unknown. No one agrees on what she looks like.
- **Imagery:** tears and masks.
- **"The Martyr's tears":** scholars and theologians interpret the falling [[Moon Shards]] as her tears. It is an interpretation, argued over in the Levont, not a known fact.
- **The lake of tears:** the salt pan below the Veingold uplands is called *Deorloen*, Elvish for "lake of tears" (Darlon to humans). Her church would read it as hers ([[Rivers of the Levont]]).
- **Tenet:** "the needs of the many". Her own death is the founding example.
- **Church:** a church of sacrifice and charity. Cool toward the [[Order of the Rising Sun]] (7.5).

## Domains (decided 31 Oct)
- **Main:** Moon, Sorrow, Duty, Protection.
- **Alternates:** Star, Repose, Void (the gap where the rest of her should be).
- PF2e has no Sacrifice or Community domain (answers 6.2). Duty is the closest to "the needs of the many".
- **Suggestion (not canon):** if she keeps Protection, [[The Sunturner]] can drop it (helps 4.1).
- **Suggestion (not canon): cracked spells.** Her powers come through her half-made mantle cracked, so a spell sometimes costs the caster something.

## Her tapestry: the Still Lake (decided 31 Oct)
Her half-working tapestry. Like every tapestry, it embodies its essence rather than containing it ([[The Tapestries]]). It replaces the 26 Oct working name, the Half-Woven.
- **Rule:** nothing there is whole. Whatever enters gives up a part of itself, and what it gives holds the realm together.
- **Image:** a black, still lake reflecting a whole, unbroken moon, while the sky above holds only bare loom threads. Half the shore is woven silver; the other half is warp threads hanging over nothing.
- **Who lives there:** almost nothing. Masked figures, half-made like her. At the heart hangs her **unfinished mantle**; whoever reaches it might finish it, a prize [[The Gods of Desirenthropy]] would want.

### Near its thin spots, and on moon nights ([[The Veil]])
- **Reflections show things whole:** a cracked jug, a lost arm.
- **Pain can pass** from one person to another who is willing to take it.
- **Tears dry to silver.**

### Her shadow
Her mantle bends her toward giving herself away. Near her, people sacrifice far too much for far too little, and her church finds it easy to ask the same of others (5.2).

## Reading it together (suggestions, not canon)
- **The veil may be her.** If her essence walls the tapestries off from the world, [[The Veil]] could be what remains of her. A thin veil is her wearing thin. Cultists of [[The Gods of Desirenthropy]] who thin it on purpose are tearing at a dead goddess.
- **Her form is unknown because she has none now.** She is a moon in pieces and a wall between worlds.
- **The gods of desirenthropy have a personal grievance with her.** She is the reason they were sealed away.
- **The railroad burns her.** Every shard in a furnace is a piece of the goddess, which her church may see as sacrilege.

## Connections
