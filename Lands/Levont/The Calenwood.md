# The Calenwood

A deep, thick wood filled with a fog that rarely lifts, in **slot C**, where the eastern range meets the sea ([[Levont Area Map]]). It used to sit beside [[Merelon]]; when Merelon moved to slot B (12 Nov 2026), **the Calenwood and its druids stayed here**, to give this corner of the Levont something of its own.

## What we know
- **The name:** Elvish *Coellchaillen*, "veiled wood" (*coell* "wood" + *caillen* "veil", softened). Humans say "the Calenwood", "veil-wood wood", the same doubling as "the Mallon River".
- **The fog is a thin spot.** Wander far enough in and you can cross into [[The Beast Tapestry]], the Levont's own fey wilds: talking beastfolk, dire animals, and time that runs wrong. Most who cross come back. Some come back changed ([[The Veil]]: time in a realm mutates).
- **The druid circle,** the [[Calenwood Circle]]: a chapter of [[Tapio]]'s faith, led by its druids, with [[Green Wardens]] as its arm. They keep people out and beasts in, and find those who wander off.
- **Why the wood is so thick:** perhaps the thin spot itself. The trees grow larger here, the way animals do near a bestial realm.
- **Distance:** about 437 km from the capital, the farthest corner of the Levont.

## Dyklan and the Giants' Dyke (decided 12 Nov)
- **The Calenwood lies beyond the Giants' Dyke,** in the pass between slot C's two ranges and on the coast behind them. The Dyke, a giant-built wall, closes the pass; the wall country and the new seventh county, [[Dyklan]], lie on the Levont side.
- **Giants still live on the far side,** among old ruins in the forest. Most now live on the tapestry side, and some venture out to the ruins, where Levont people may fight them.
- **The roars** heard from the fog are the giants calling for their lost heir.
- *Suggestion:* the fog land behind the Dyke is about 2,500 to 3,000 km², about the size of Luxembourg.
