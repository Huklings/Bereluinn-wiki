# Faelath

The hunter of the [[Elven Pantheon]]. Neutral, but darker. Named 29 Oct 2026.

## The name
Elvish, from Gaelic *faol*, "wolf", plus *-ath*, "place of": roughly "wolf-land", or "where the wolf is" ([[Languages and Naming#Elvish (direction 19 Oct, kit approved 22 Oct)]]). Borrowing check: Sindarin has a word *fael* ("fair-minded"), but the match is faint.

## Decided (29 Oct)
- **A hunter goddess, neutral but darker.** She is female (29 Oct).
- **All elves worship Faelath, but they are cautious about invoking her.**
- **The [[Finelor]] first worshipped Faelath,** then left her to join the orderly side.
- **The [[Northern Elves]] worship Faelath.**
- **Fell out of favour.** For blessings in battle, the [[Aelnor]] turned to [[The Sunturner]] instead, a more benevolent warrior goddess.
- **Domains (29 Oct):** Might, Freedom, Death, Destruction, Nature. The strong taking, the unbound wild, the kill, and what the hunt leaves behind. (Darkness was considered and dropped.)

## Her tapestry: the Red Woods (decided 30 Oct)
Like every tapestry, it embodies its essence rather than containing it: a realm where the hunt is everywhere and stronger ([[The Tapestries]]).
- **Rule:** everything is hunter or hunted.
- **Image:** endless red-leaved forest under a red dusk that never ends, with horns somewhere far off.
- **Who lives there:** the great beasts and her wolves. If "she keeps what she kills" is kept (below), the souls she has taken run as her pack. [[Tapio]]'s beast tapestry is wild change; hers is only the hunt.

### Near its thin spots ([[The Veil]])
- **Predators grow bolder and bigger.**
- **Fresh tracks appear** with no animal to make them.
- **Anything wounded there is followed.**
- **The dusk lingers** too long.

### Her shadow
Her mantle has bent her into a hunt that cannot stop: she cannot spare anything she has started to chase.

## Suggestions (not canon)
- **Iconography:** a wolf, or a wolf's head. A grey cloak, a bow of bone, eyes in the treeline. Elves may not carve her face, only her tracks.
- **She keeps what she kills.** The northern elves learned their soul-binding from her, which is why other elves invoke her so carefully.
- **The Finelor's ordeal** may have been the price of leaving her ([[Finelor]], 10.6).
