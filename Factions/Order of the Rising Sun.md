# Order of the Rising Sun

A rich, armored knightly aristocracy serving [[The Sunturner]]. It is the remnant of a once-rich human border kingdom, holds the northern marches of [[The March]], and commands a private army the council does not control. It makes its money by solving problems, whether or not anyone asked, and then sending the bill.

## Name and motto
The name ties the Order to [[The Sunturner]] and her Ambition domain: as the sun rises, so does our ambition. Her title, from the flower that keeps turning toward the sun, fits the motto too. A motto in that spirit (wording not final): *"The sun rises, and so do we."*

## What we know
- **Origin:** the remnant of an old, rich border kingdom of humans, with no elves in it. The Sunturner herself came from that kingdom before she ascended. Its wealth is now held by the Order rather than the region.
- **Wealth:** paid work as soldiers and knights. Highly educated soldiers march in, take charge and solve the problem, sometimes without being asked, then demand payment for solving a hard problem. They are not above looting.
- **Ranks:** senior members all hold knightly ranks. Below them is the support staff: squires, servants and the like.
- **Signature weapon:** the axe. Because axes are cool.
- **Look:** black armor, so the sun symbols stand out. Visual references: Nilfgaardian armor (The Witcher) and the Knights of the Blazing Sun (Warhammer). See [[Borrowed Ideas]].
- **Territory (decided 22 Oct):** the **northern marches**, the northern stretch of [[The March]], from the rocky coast down to just north of the main gate. **The Order does not hold the main gate.** Earlier notes placed its lands in the north and east; that no longer applies. See the [[Levont Area Map]] (marked N).

### The northern marches
**Decided 22 Oct:**
- **It holds the north, not the gate.** The main gate, the busiest pass and its tolls belong to the southern marches. The Order guards the quieter stretch of wall.
- **That's its grievance.** The glory, the tolls and most of the fighting go through a gate it doesn't hold.
- **So it sallies south.** When the main gate or another pass is attacked, the Order rides down to "help", whether or not anyone asked. After the fighting it presents a bill: payment for its expertise and skill. The Order calls it a fair fee. The southern lords, the counties and the council call it extortion. This is the Order's old habit of uninvited problem-solving followed by a bill (7.2), now with a frontier to practise it on.

**The back country (25 Oct):** its home is [[Suncourt]], in slot G behind the northern marches. Its hall, **the Tribunal**, is its seat and court. Beside it lie its training grounds, the festival town of Gildhem, whose gambling houses the Order owns, and the falconers who watch the rocky coast. To the south is the Glasswood, a preserve the Order wants to hunt in ([[Levont Area Map]]).

**Suggestions, not canon:**
- **Why it fits its history:** the old border kingdom *was* the Levont's western frontier. At the founding, the counties were drawn across the old borders, and the southern stretch of its march, gate and all, went to new union nobles. The Order kept the north and its wealth, and has resented losing the gate ever since.
- **Two kinds of marcher, one wall:** old Frankish knights with their own justice in the north, union-made lords with charter liberties in the south.
- **Why the council tolerates it:** the Order sits a few days from the gate, while a full levy takes 4 to 8 weeks to muster and march ([[World Scale]]). An expensive rescuer that turns up is better than a cheap one that doesn't.

**Hooks (suggestions):**
- **The price list.** The Order keeps a written schedule of fees: so much per keep relieved, per raider killed, per day in the field. Southern lords argue over every line of it.
- **Arriving too late on purpose.** A southern lord swears the Order waited on the ridge until his keep was nearly lost, so the bill would be bigger.
- **The lord who won't pay.** One southern lord refuses. Next time his pass is attacked, will the Order ride?
- **The invitation.** A desperate southern lord sends for the Order in writing, and the council treats it as treason against the union's chain of command.
- **The council pays.** To keep the peace, the council quietly settles the Order's bills from the treasury, which the counties find out about.
- **Faith:** many Knights are devout, but few ever develop paladin-like magic.
- **Membership:** began all-human. Now admits sun elves ([[Aelnor]]) and half-elves.
- **Power:** a private army, its own justice, and judicial deference at national level.
- **Founder claim:** among the first to join the gods, so it may argue it is a party to the Levont, not a subject of it.
- **Its founding (decided 22 Oct):** the Order began about 600 years ago, in [[The Sundering]], when knights of the human border kingdom followed one of their own, the wizard who would ascend as [[The Sunturner]]. The kingdom outlived the war and later became one of the old countries. So the Order is older than the Levont and serves a goddess who was once one of its people.
- **Emergency powers:** wartime powers that nobody formally revoked.

## Inside the Order
| Group | What they want |
| --- | --- |
| Old guard | To keep the old kingdom's human culture in charge. They currently outweigh the new guard |
| New guard | Sun elves, half-elves and newer members who want an equal place |
| Squires | To be raised to knighthood |
| Support staff | To be paid |

## The council and the Order
The council keeps a strenuous hold on the Order. The Order knows open rebellion would bring too many soldiers down on it, and the two usually find their interests line up. A standoff where both sides behave, for now.

## Rivals and relations
Relationships across the setting will get their own web later.
- [[The Martyr]]'s church: cool rather than hostile (7.5).
- **The southern marcher lords:** neighbours on the same wall. They hold the main gate the Order wants, and they're the ones who get its bills (decided 22 Oct). See [[The March]].
- [[Green Wardens]]: probably rivals. The Order may want to hunt in forests the Wardens protect, and anyone who over-hunts draws the Wardens' ire (13.7).
- [[Dwarves]]: both rank-conscious (11.15).
- [[Former Countries of the Levont]]: descends from the old border kingdom.
