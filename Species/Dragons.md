# Dragons

Enslavers of the [[Dwarves]]. Still active, but few.

## What we know
- Dragons enslaved the dwarves. Each dragon used its dwarves differently, which may define the clans (11.4).
- They are a candidate for the other side of [[The Sundering]], but the text doesn't say so.

## Decided 26 Oct
- **Dragons are active**, but like many great monsters they don't exist in the hundreds of thousands. They are rare.
- **They most likely fought the [[Giants]]**, much as in D&D and Pathfinder 2e lore, **using their enslaved dwarven armies.**

## What follows (suggestions, not canon)
- **Each dragon is a known individual,** with a name, a territory and a long memory. One waking or moving is a political event for the Levont, not a random encounter.
- **Part of the dwarf–giant war was never the dwarves' own.** The giants may hate them for it anyway, because the dwarves held the axes.
- **Older than the gods' realms.** Dragons may still draw on loose magic the old way ([[Magic#How magic is restricted (decided 26 Oct)]]).
- **They remember who served them.** What does a living dragon think of free dwarf clans?

## Possible link (suggestion only)
[[areas of the Levont]], Area 5: a "grandiose and devious being" is locked in an elden dwarven hold, tied to domination and greed. That could be a dragon.

## What else we know
- Some very old dragons may have witnessed [[The Apotheosis]]. If so, they now live with the gods in [[The Tapestries]].
