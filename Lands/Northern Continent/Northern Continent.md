# Northern Continent

A continent to the north, drawn on the map but not yet named. "Northern Continent" is a placeholder until it has one.

## What we know
- Home of the [[Northern Elves]], the remnants of the elves who joined the betrayer gods ([[The Gods of Desirenthropy]]).

## Open questions

- Who else lives there besides the northern elves?
- How do people travel between it and the Levont, and does anyone?
