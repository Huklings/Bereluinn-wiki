# The Elden Era

The age before the present. It ends in [[The Sundering]], the war whose ending brought the world into [[The Current Era]], with many gods ascending in [[The Apotheosis]].

## What we know
- **It lasted thousands of years and ended about 600 years ago** with the Sundering (decided 22 Oct, see [[Timeline]]). It sits in the mostly-forgotten layer of history.
- Places from this age are called "elden": the elden elven cities of the marshes, the Elden human city under the plains' county seat, and the elden dwarven hold in the valley ([[areas of the Levont]]).
- "The war of elden eras" salted the earth of Area 3, turning forest and valley into steppe.
- The **Moonshard Calamity** happened in this age, during the war, and killed a god ([[The Moon's Shattering]]).
- **There were no gods in this age (26 Oct), only wizard-lords** with uncontained power, whom ordinary people called gods. Each race was made by a lord: [[Ishgadrum]] made the [[Dwarves]]. See [[Pantheon#Where the gods came from (decided 26 Oct)]].
- The name has changed between drafts. "Elden" is the current one.
