# Angels

Souls that have been changed by long exposure to a god's tapestry. Angels are not a separate creation. They are what a soul becomes after enough time in a realm.

## What we know
- Spending time in a tapestry mutates any being ([[The Veil]]).
- Angels are the souls of beings mutated by a god's tapestry.

## What follows (suggestions, not canon)
- **Souls go to "whatever god has claimed them"** ([[Death and the Afterlife]]). Over time, the faithful dead may become that god's angels.
- **The same process works in every realm.** Souls in the tapestries of [[The Gods of Desirenthropy]] would change too. What mortals call angels and what they call fiends may be one phenomenon in different realms, which fits the gray pantheon.
- **Each god's angels would look like that god's realm:** sunlit in the Sunturner's, bestial in a primal one.

## Possible angels (suggestion)
Very old elves and dragons who witnessed [[The Apotheosis]] may live in the tapestries. After so long, they may have been changed into angels themselves.
