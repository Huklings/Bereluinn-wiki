# Species of the Levont

The peoples of the [[Levont]]. Working assumption: species cut across the old borders, and each region has a majority plus at least two significant minorities (2.3). Avoid "dwarves get mountains, elves get forest".

- [[Aelnor]]: sun-touched elves whose kingdom fell.
- [[Finelor]]: horned, secluded elves, descended from defectors.
- [[Dwarves]]: thirteen clans, made by [[Ishgadrum]], once enslaved by [[Dragons]].
- Humans: no notes yet.
- Beyond the Levont: the [[Northern Elves]] of the northern continent.
- Beyond the Levont: the [[Toad People]] of the island continent to the south, a foul species that raids the coasts (26 Oct).
- In the mountains: [[Giants]], five or six kinds with strong ties to the tapestries (26 Oct).
- [[Halffolk]]: the **Wendfolk**, one small people, mostly in the country, with some (the Tonfolk) in the cities. A magical luck that runs on food (28 Oct). Their home is [[Wendmark]].

## Cross-border institutions to consider
A dwarven mining guild, an elven law order (2.3).
