# The Pale Wardens

A [[Finelor]] faction that surveys thin spots in [[The Veil]]. Added 25 Oct 2026 as a **draft with potential but not enough spice yet**. You plan to add your own input. Nothing here is canon beyond the core idea.

## The name
*Finchaillen*, "pale veil" (*fin* "pale, bright" + *caillen* "veil", softened). Working name; English "the Pale Wardens".

## The core idea
The Finelor's secret ordeal ([[Finelor]]) seems to let them walk into thin spots without changing as fast as others. So they are the most accessible people who can survey the veil properly: where a thin spot lies, how thin it is, and which realm is behind it. The council needs them; the public still shuns them. A stigmatised people with a monopoly.

## Decided (12 Nov 2026)
- **Not only the Finelor can find thin spots.** Others can calculate or sense where the veil is thin, but they are so rare that the Finelor are **the most accessible source**. That is why the council turns to them.
- **Their gift is fading.** Their sense for thin spots is the last of [[Faelath]]'s hunting gift. It fades as the horns do ([[Finelor#Turning back into elves (decided 12 Nov 2026)]]), so every generation has fewer who can survey.
- **Every survey risks re-dyeing them.** A thin spot onto the Red Woods can push a surveyor back toward Faelath. One onto [[Athréth]]'s Waiting Wood washes them faster, and takes the sense with it.

### Suggestions (not canon)
- **Who else can do it:** [[Giants]] feel seams (canon), a few mages may calculate them (perhaps at Cloesolin, [[Merelon]]), and old elves who remember past lives may know old ones. All rare, slow or unwilling.
- **Do both sides of the Finelor debate serve?** The Wardens are the Keeping's strongest argument: the Levont needs a gift that is dying out.
- **Folklore:** "Pale line, cold hearth": families sweep a house clean before a surveyor comes, so the veil "finds nothing to hold".
- **Hooks:** an old surveyor's map has been wrong for twenty years, on purpose; a count hires the party to find a rare mage who can check a Finelor survey; a surveyor comes back from a Red Woods thin spot more beastlike than they went in.

## Earlier spice ideas (25 Oct, suggestions)
- **Nobody can check their work.** Only a Finelor can survey a thin spot, so only a Finelor can say a survey is wrong. Their maps decide which preserves stay and which villages move ([[Preserves and Parks#The borders don't follow the veil (decided 25 Oct)]]). A false survey could empty a valley or condemn one.
- **Every survey costs them.** They aren't immune, only slower to change. Each walk into a thin spot adds a little more: longer fingers, a new ridge on the horns, a dream that won't stop. The oldest surveyors barely look elven.
- **They are bribed and blamed.** Landowners pay for "clean" surveys. Villages that lose their homes curse the pale surveyor who drew the line.
- **The ordeal must be renewed.** If the protection fades with each generation, some Finelor go back to the ordeal on purpose to stay useful, and no one will say where it happens.
- **They know where Birdsong works.** A cult thinning the veil on purpose leaves marks only a Pale Warden could read ([[Merelon]]).
