# Masters of the Larhouse

**Named 12 Nov 2026: they are simply the masters** (the masters of Cloesolin), a plain teacher's title, with no special name. **Common people call them "the masters of the hall"** (12 Nov). This note keeps its old filename so links don't break; rename it in Obsidian to "Masters of Cloesolin" if you like, and Obsidian will update the links.

The governing body of the Larhouse, the Levont's licensed academy of magic in [[Merelon]]. Mixed species, with many [[Aelnor]] scholars. Added 25 Oct 2026. **The idea is decided; the name still needs work.**

**Changed 12 Nov:** the name "Larhouse" is dropped, and the school is one of at least two licensed schools, founded by [[Wizard Houses]]. **The school is now named Cloesolin, the Hall of Lamps** (common folk: Saltmere Hall), decided 12 Nov ([[Merelon]]). The masters' own name still waits; *Masters of Cloesolin* is a working label, suggestion only. The candidates below are kept for reference; the Larmoot shares a root with Witan, which you turned down.

## The lamps (decided 12 Nov)
- **Each new mage of Cloesolin is given a lamp.** When a master dies or loses their licence, their lamp in the hall is put out.
- **It is an uncommon, inward ritual.** Common people do not know about the lamps; only those in the know do (mages, and the few who deal closely with the hall). To Merelon folk it is just Saltmere Hall.

### Suggestions (not canon)
- **Hooks:** a dark lamp is found burning again; a mage's lamp goes out while they are still alive.
- **Borrowing check:** light as learning is common everywhere; nothing close to one work.

## Name candidates
- **The Larmoot:** common tongue *lār* "lore" + *mōt* "assembly", like the Anglo-Saxon *witenagemot*.
- **The Stone Bench:** after the elden standing stones in the Larhouse grounds.
- **An Elvish name** for the older, elven-led faction inside it: *Cloesola*, "stone light" (*cloe* + *sola*).

## What it is
- **It speaks for every licensed caster** in the Levont, through a council seat ([[Government of the Levont]]).
- **It wants fewer limits** on magic, and the churches want more. The gods' limits are the faith's teaching; the masters treat them as a technical problem ([[Magic]]).
- **It controls who may practise.** A licence comes from the Larhouse, and graduates owe the council years of service.

## Suggestions (not canon)
- **Shadow:** Birdsong grows inside the Larhouse, and the masters would rather not know ([[Merelon]]).
- **Rivals:** the churches; the [[Calenwood Circle|Calenwood circle]] (no longer next door since 12 Nov); unlicensed hedge casters.
