# The Bud and the Sprout

Neutral god (or gods) of newborns and orchards.

## What we know
- The title names two, but the text describes only the Bud.
- Allows only new-body resurrection.

## One god, two aspects (decided 31 Oct)
- **The Bud and the Sprout are two aspects of one god.** People believe they are two different gods, and see one as far more benevolent than the other.
- **The Bud:** closed potential, the seed that waits.
- **The Sprout:** the breaking-out, reckless and fragile.
- **What it owns:** the raw urge to begin, and how wasteful that is. Nature here is not a kind green mother: a tree drops a million seeds so one can grow, and almost every beginning fails. **Gods overlap (31 Oct):** domains align like a Venn diagram, so this god can share growth, nature and forest with [[Greenhand]], [[Tapio]], [[Faelath]] and [[Lady Bluecloak]]. Each just weights them differently.
- **Domains:** Nature, Family, Change, Swarm. Alternates: Decay, Healing, Plague. Healing and Plague mirror its two aspects: the kinder face heals, the harsher one brings the storm-sickness (31 Oct).

## Its tapestry: the Seedfall (decided 31 Oct)
Like every tapestry, it embodies its essence rather than containing it ([[The Tapestries]]). It replaces the 26 Oct draft, the Orchard.
- **Rule:** growth and birth are abundant, and everything strives to live.
- **Image:** wild plains and forest, occasionally struck by great pollen storms that roll in like weather. After a storm, everything bursts: insect swarms, blooms, births.
- **Who lives there:** everything young and striving, by the million.

### Near its thin spots ([[The Veil]])
- **Pollen storms pass through the veil (31 Oct).** They drive growth and disease alike. If you're lucky, a storm only makes your crops grow faster. If you're unlucky, it brings mice and sickness, because sickness is natural too.
- **Blooms out of season,** seeds sprouting in stone.
- **Swarms:** mayflies, locusts, rats. Unlike [[Greenhand]]'s healthy doubled births, this is a flood, and most of it dies.

### Its shadow
It loves beginnings and doesn't care about endings. Its faithful accept that most newborns die, which clashes with [[The Sunturner]]'s Rebuke Death (9.18). It is also why it allows only new-body resurrection: life goes forward, never back.

## Suggestions (not canon)
- **Which aspect is "the kind one"?** Perhaps the Bud: patron of pregnancies and nurseries, prayed to for a safe birth. The Sprout gets the blame for the swarms and the cradle deaths. Two churches could grow from the one god.
- **Orchards move to [[Greenhand]],** since an orchard is tended.
