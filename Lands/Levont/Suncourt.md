# Suncourt

The home of the [[Order of the Rising Sun]], in the back country behind the northern marches. Its hall, **the Tribunal**, is where the Order governs, judges and keeps its books. Around it lie the Order's manors and training grounds, a festival town built to take the knights' pay back off them, and to the south a wood still warped by the old catastrophe. Area 8, reworked from an old draft (added 25 Oct 2026).

**What changed from the old draft:** the draft came from the time when the Levont was one city with districts. The Order no longer comes from the capital: it comes from the old border kingdom ([[Former Countries of the Levont]]). It doesn't guard gold mines, which are Veingold country. The orcs are reached overland, not across an archipelago ([[Lorweir]]). The military parade belongs to the plains (Area 6), and trained birds for captains to the marshes (Area 4), so those were dropped or changed.

## Where
**Slot G**, the northwest interior between the March, the plains, the heartland and the rocky coast ([[Levont Area Map]]). About 174 km from the capital, and a few days' ride from the main gate. The Pren and its streams run through it to a small harbour on the north coast ([[Rivers of the Levont]]).

## The name
Old Frankish, the old border kingdom's tongue ([[Languages and Naming#The Levont's human tongues (template, 19 Oct)]]): *sunna* "sun" + *-court* "estate". The court of the sun. Older documents give the Frankish spelling, *Sunnacourt*.

## The Tribunal
The Order's guild hall, seat and court in one.
- **Its own justice:** the Order judges its own members here, under its own law. Knights who break the code stand before the Tribunal, not a county court ([[Order of the Rising Sun#What we know]]).
- **Outsiders may bring a case,** if they pay a filing fee. The Order bills even for justice.
- **The books:** the Order's schedule of fees, the bills sent south after every sally, and the debts lords owe it are all kept here. Whoever holds the Tribunal's ledgers knows who in the Levont is in the Order's pocket.
- **The relic:** if the Order keeps a relic of [[The Sunturner]] from before she ascended, it's kept here.

## The training grounds
- Squires are raised to knighthood here, and the northern marches are where they earn it. A field promotion on the wall is worth more than any ceremony.
- Manors and timber yards support the Order behind the line.

## Gildhem, the festival town
A town of feasts, games, gambling and tournaments next to Suncourt, where knights on leave spend their pay. *Gildhem* is Frankish-style for "pay home" (*gild* "payment" + *-hem* "home").
- **The Order owns the gambling houses.** The gold goes round in a circle: the Order pays its knights, and Gildhem takes it back.
- **Why it exists:** bored, rich, armed young men need somewhere to go that isn't a neighbour's village. Gildhem keeps the peace by keeping them busy.
- **Tournaments** are the Order's display of power ([[Worldbuilding Sage Lessons#Feudal contracts and succession]]). Southern marcher lords who come to joust are also being watched.

## The coast watch
The Order's **falconers** patrol the rocky coast and its islands, the northern end of the Order's lands. Their falcons scout and harass raiders, smugglers' boats and anything coming from the north sea. They are the Order's eyes on the water.
- **Rivals in the sky:** the marsh county's red-winged crows serve captains on the other side of the Levont. Crow-handlers and falconers despise each other's birds.

## The Glasswood
A great wood south of Suncourt, still shrouded by strange magic from the old catastrophe.
- **What happened:** the Moon Shard Storm came down here ([[The Moon's Shattering]]). The trees grew back around craters of fused, glassy stone, which is where the name comes from.
- **A preserve:** the [[Green Wardens]] guard it ([[Preserves and Parks]]). The Order wants to hunt in it. The two have argued over it for generations.
- **What lives there:** veil-touched beasts drawn to old shard falls, and maybe new falls, since shards seem to land where they've landed before.
