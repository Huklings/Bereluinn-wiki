# The Sundering

The name for the whole war at the end of [[The Elden Era]], and for the way it ended. Wizard-lords, whom ordinary people already called gods, fought, a moon broke, the Martyr died, magic was bound into mantles, and the world came out of it into [[The Current Era]].

## What we know
- **About 600 years ago** (decided 22 Oct, see [[Timeline]]). The dwarves were freed as the dragons fell.
- **It was not good against evil.** The wizards split over the tapestry plan, and the other side was a mix of several groups. See [[Sides of the Sundering]].
- **The cause (26 Oct):** the elden lords were destroying the land for their creations and threatening the moons. One side invented the mantle ritual to rein them in ([[Pantheon#Where the gods came from (decided 26 Oct)]]).
- In [[The Martyr]]'s myth, the gods fought and the weakest bound the others to their domains.
- Mortal wizard-lords ascended to become the good gods ([[The Apotheosis]]).
- The [[Aelnor]] fought alongside the good gods "centuries ago".
- The [[Finelor]] descend from those who defected to the good side.
- **The Moonshard Calamity:** [[The Martyr]] chose to die, and her shattered essence created the tapestries and walled them off. A moon broke ([[The Moon's Shattering]]).
- The gods broke and weakened [[Magic]] and limited the path to godhood, as part of how the war ended.
- The gods then withdrew into [[The Tapestries]].
- Wartime emergency powers were never formally revoked.
- The [[Order of the Rising Sun]] was among the first to join the gods: its knightly founders followed the wizard who would ascend as [[The Sunturner]]. She came from their human border kingdom, which outlived the war (decided 22 Oct).
- [[Lady Bluecloak]] contained elemental energies, possibly during the war.
- [[The Contest of the 41 Pillars]] is set "after the sundering", meaning after the war.

## Effects on the land
- [[areas of the Levont]], Area 3: the war of elden eras salted the earth, turning forest and valley into steppe.
- The [[Moon Shards]] still fall from the broken moon's debris belt.
