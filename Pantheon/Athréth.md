# Athréth

The elves' maker. An elden wizard-lord who made the elves, made them die after about 400 years, and gave them rebirth in return ([[Death and the Afterlife#Elves are reborn (decided 26 Oct)]]). Named 29 Oct 2026.

## The name
Elvish, from Gaelic *athbhreith*, "rebirth" ([[Languages and Naming#Elvish (direction 19 Oct, kit approved 22 Oct)]]).

## Decided
- **Made the elves** (26 Oct), as [[Ishgadrum]] made the dwarves.
- **Named Athréth** (29 Oct).
- **He is male** (29 Oct).
- **Domains (30 Oct):** Change, Soul, Repose, Family. Alternate: Dreams. Rebirth, the soul going round again, and the elves as one long family. Can be revisited later.

## His tapestry: the Waiting Wood (decided 30 Oct)
Like every tapestry, it embodies its essence rather than containing it: a realm where return is everywhere and stronger ([[The Tapestries]]).
- **Rule:** nothing ends; it waits to return.
- **Image:** a still, golden forest where every resting elf soul stands as a tree, families growing in rings. When a soul is reborn, its tree pulls up its roots and is gone.
- **Who lives there:** quiet tenders, and the waiting souls themselves, half-dreaming. The [[Northern Elves]]' trees stand grey and leafless.

### Near its thin spots ([[The Veil]])
- **Death doesn't stick.** Stumps regrow, burnt ground greens within a season, lost things turn up again.
- **A sapling springs up where an elf died.** No elf will cut one.
- **Elves feel the pull of past lives:** they recognise faces and places they have never seen.
- **Pale seed-down drifts wherever someone is mourned.**

### His shadow
His mantle has bent him toward waiting until he cannot let anything go (the way a Shard's Intent bends its Vessel, in Sanderson's Cosmere). That is why the northern elves' grey trees still stand.

## The Airden (decided 13 Nov 2026)
- **He and his apprentices made the Airden,** the first elves, deep in [[The Elden Era]], a bit after the dwarves. Several apprentices later became gods, such as [[Gaesín]] and [[Faelath]]. Together they are the Cirenoth ([[Elven Pantheon#The Cirenoth (decided 13 Nov 2026)]]).
- **His circle was proud** and gave the Airden far more raw creation magic than other peoples. The other wizard-lords stepped in when the Airden marched for land: some say the first true wizard war.
- **The rework.** Spent, the Cirenoth retreated, limited elven numbers and reworked the elves: less magic, more numerous.
- **Kin trees** hold the magic cut out of the Airden. Their roots reach his Waiting Wood, and a soul is reborn only near a living kin tree.
- **The rework is a wound** for some elves, above all the [[Northern Elves]], who already distrust their maker.
- Full summary: [[Aelnor#The Airden (decided 13 Nov 2026)]].
