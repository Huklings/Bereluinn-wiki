# Heleriam Aeloven

Sun elf master mason and smith. **The name** (22 Oct): *Heleriam* is a given name with the men's ending *-iam*; *Aeloven* is a house name, *ael* "sun-warmed" + *ben* "house", softened to *-ven*: "sun house". In full: *Heleriam ela Aeloven*, "Heleriam of the house of Aeloven" ([[Languages and Naming]]). He built the pillars in [[The Contest of the 41 Pillars]] and was killed afterward. His son avenged him.
