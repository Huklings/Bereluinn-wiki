# Lurian

Neutral god of secrecy and archives. The Keeper of Hidden Lore.

## What we know
- **Symbols (unsettled):** the sealed scroll, or a closed tome.
- **Imagery:** hooded figure with candles and tomes.
- **Domains (draft):** Silence and Mysteries may be homebrew (9.15).

## Possible role
His clergy could hold the real story of [[The Founding of the Levont]] or [[The Apotheosis]]. They might also hold the record of the [[Finelor]]'s ordeal, or the true account of [[The Contest of the 41 Pillars]].

## Connections
- [[areas of the Levont]]: Area 4's hidden vault of tomes could be one of his archives.
