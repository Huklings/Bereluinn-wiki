# Calenwood Circle

The druid circle of [[The Calenwood]], the foggy wood in slot C at the far east of the Levont. (Until 12 Nov the wood sat beside [[Merelon]]; Merelon moved to slot B, and the wood and its druids stayed.) A chapter of [[Tapio]]'s faith, led by its druids, with [[Green Wardens]] as its arm. **Not a council seat.** Added 25 Oct 2026.

## What it does
- **Guards the fog.** The Calenwood is a thin spot onto [[The Beast Tapestry]]. The circle keeps people out and beasts in, and finds the people who wander off.
- **Distrusts casters.** It distrusts anyone working magic beside a thin place, and the schools of magic distrust druids who tell them where they may work. (Rewritten 12 Nov: the school is no longer next door.)

## The beastfolk are a fairy tale
To almost everyone in the Levont, the talking beastfolk of the tapestry are bedtime stories: badgers in waistcoats, wolf-kings, tea with a hare. Nobody would give them a voice on the council, and nobody thinks they need one. **The circle knows better**: its druids have met them in the fog. They keep quiet, because saying so in public would get them laughed at, or worse.

## Suggestions (not canon)
- A druid who once spoke up about the beastfolk was mocked out of the order. Some think he's still in the fog.
- Local youths dare each other to walk in "to see the rabbits". Most come back with nothing. Some come back too quiet.
