# Side Pantheons

Smaller groups of gods whose domains share a common goal. A side pantheon can mix good and evil gods, and one god can belong to several. Mortals often name these groups, and they can worship a whole group at once. [[The Lawful Accords]] may be only one such grouping, perhaps a name mortals gave it.

The model is Pathfinder 2e's pantheons, such as Sorrow's Sword: five gods of vengeance and reclaimed homelands, worshipped together, whose followers are lawful good, chaotic good or chaotic neutral.

## How side pantheons work
- **United by a goal, not by alignment.** The members share a purpose. Their reasons and methods can differ sharply.
- **Overlapping membership.** A god of the Accords can belong to other side pantheons too, including ones that also hold a god of [[The Gods of Desirenthropy]].
- **Named by mortals.** Many groupings are what worshippers call the gods they pray to together. The gods may not see themselves as a group at all.
- **Worshipped as a group.** A cleric can serve a whole side pantheon, following its shared edicts and anathema (this fits Pathfinder 2e pantheon rules).

## What each side pantheon needs
| Field | What to decide |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Usually the name mortals use |
| Shared goal | The one purpose all members serve |
| Members | Which gods, and from which side |
| Followers | Which alignments the worshippers can be |
| Edicts | What followers must do |
| Anathema | What followers must never do |
| Who worships it | Which people, orders or regions |

## Possible side pantheons from existing gods (suggestions, not canon)
Working descriptions, not names.
- **The keepers of passage:** gods of death and what follows. [[The Smiling Father]], [[The Bud and the Sprout]], and [[Lusitri]]'s Soul domain. Shared goal: that the dead reach the right place. They disagree bitterly about resurrection, which a shared pantheon would have to live with.
- ~~**The wild circle**~~: decided 31 Oct, above.
- **The bound word:** [[Verdan Ironoath]], [[Lurian]] and perhaps a god of desirenthropy who trades in bargains. Shared goal: that promises and secrets are kept. A good god and a greedy one in the same pantheon shows the gray pantheon at work.
- **The ambitious:** [[The Sunturner]] (Ambition) with a god of desirenthropy who never accepted the limits. Shared goal: rise. Since few people even know such pairings exist, this would be a fringe idea among outcast scholars.
