# Divine Essences

Each god stored their power in an essence. When a god dies, the essence remains, and someone else can take up the mantle. Godhood can therefore pass from one holder to the next, and anyone can now ascend.

## What we know
- **The mantle ritual (26 Oct).** The side that wanted to rein in the wizard-lords created a ritual that bound loose magic to each lord as a divine mantle. Holding a mantle gives domain over a tapestry and lets the holder live essentially forever, though they can be killed ([[Pantheon#Where the gods came from (decided 26 Oct)]]). This note's "essence" is the power held in that mantle.
- **Taking a mantle means finding the essence.** There is no chosen one.
- **Killing a god releases the essence**, so whoever kills a god can take it.
- Anyone can ascend now, not only the wizard-lords of [[The Elden Era]].
- **The gods have limited the path.** They weakened and broke [[Magic]] and restricted how anyone can ascend. That is why the world isn't full of gods.

## What it touches (suggestions, not canon)
- **The dead god** (answered). [[The Martyr]] chose to die while making her mantle, before it fully materialised. The fragments are the [[Moon Shards]], and her tapestry only half works. Could enough shards be shaped into a new essence?
- **Why the gods withdrew.** If killing a god opens a path to godhood, the gods are targets. [[The Tapestries]] protect the world from the gods, and the gods from the world.
- **[[The Smiling Father]].** His Fate domain says even gods must pass on. Succession of mantles fits that doctrine.
- **[[The Martyr]]'s unknown form.** No one agrees what the Martyr looks like. Several holders over the ages would explain that.
- **[[The Sunturner]]'s appearance by region (4.4).** Regional versions could remember different holders.
- **[[The Sunturner]]'s Ambition domain.** A goddess of ambition in a world where anyone can become a god.

## Sanderson's Second Law: the limits decide everything
"Anyone can" is a power. The setting's stability depends on what stops most people.
- **How does someone take a mantle?** (Answered) By finding the essence. Killing a god releases it.
- How exactly did the gods limit ascension? A barrier, a hidden location, a cost, a test?
- Does the essence change its holder? Does the new god keep their own mind, or become the old god?
- Where are essences kept: inside the god, in their tapestry, or somewhere that can be reached?
- What does the attempt cost a mortal who fails?
- Can one person hold two mantles?
- Can a new god make a new essence, or are the existing ones all there is?
