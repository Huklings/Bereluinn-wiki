# Green Wardens

The military order of [[Tapio]]'s faith. They hunt mutated creatures before one can wipe out a whole ecosystem, and they serve the public as rough-and-ready park rangers across the wilds and [[Preserves and Parks]] of the [[Levont]]. Common folk call them **green caps**, after the green hats and capes of their uniform.

**Their god's own realm (decided 31 Oct).** [[The Beast Tapestry]] is [[Tapio]]'s. The faith worships him but stops his tapestry from spilling over, keeping the balance, so many of the dire beasts and mutations the Wardens hunt come from their own god.

## What we know
- **Name:** the order is the Green Wardens. "Wardens" is the everyday short form. "Green caps" is the street nickname.
- **Purpose:** they hunt anything mutated, so that no mutated monster kills off an entire ecosystem.
- **Public face:** park rangers in all but name. They guide, track, and find the lost. Like everything in a medieval world, they are only loosely organized.
- **Membership:** open to any species in the Levont. Anyone who cares for nature can join. Not everyone can become a cleric or druid.
- **Look:** green hats and capes over **real metal armor**. Not the usual leather-and-hood ranger: a huntsmaster who means business wears armor, and so do the Wardens. "Half-knight" only describes this look: part ranger, part knight.
- **Druids and clerics** usually go without metal armor, by custom rather than by rule.
- **Scale:** national, since the preserves span the whole Levont.

## Structure
A chapter is led by a **druid or a cleric**. The Wardens are its military arm and handle military matters under that leadership.

| Rank | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Druid or cleric | Leads the chapter. Not a Warden rank, and not open to everyone |
| Warden captain | Commands the Wardens of a chapter |
| Warden | Full member |
| Initiate | New recruit |

## Funding
Money comes from several places, and each one pulls a little differently.

| Source | What it buys | What it costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A patron on the council who shares their values | Political cover and steady coin | A friend at the top who may expect favors, and enemies among that patron's rivals |
| Donations to [[Tapio]]'s clergy | The faith's backing | The church's priorities come first |
| Paid jobs from villages | Hunting beasts, investigating strange sightings on the roads | Coin depends on trouble. Poor villages get less help |

## Relations
- **Hunters:** anyone who hunts *too much* draws their ire, whoever they are.
- **[[Order of the Rising Sun]]:** probably rivals. The Order may want to hunt in forests the Wardens protect, and over-hunting by knights would bring the two into conflict.

## Connections
- [[The Veil]]: mutations come from thin places in the veil. Since a thin veil is invisible, their trackers read the signs: more fires, stranger animals, and (in the one region it affects) sleepless villages.
- [[Preserves and Parks]]: their territory.
- [[Finelor]], [[The Bud and the Sprout]]: does "mutated" ever get pointed at people?
- [[areas of the Levont]]: Area 2's Land Wardens with hunting dogs, the only armed group allowed there. The same order, or a local force?
