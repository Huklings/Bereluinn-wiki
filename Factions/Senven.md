# Senven

The Witness House: an [[Aelnor]] house of elven notaries who, by law, must witness every great oath, charter and treaty in the [[Levont]]. Elves outlive every human dynasty, so an elven witness is a witness who will still be alive when the dispute comes. Added 25 Oct 2026. **The name and core idea are decided; the details are suggestions.**

## The name
*Senven*: *sen* "old" + *ben* "house", softened to *-ven* ([[Languages and Naming#Elvish (direction 19 Oct, kit approved 22 Oct)]]). "The old house." Humans call it the Witness House, and lawyers say "a Senven seal".

## What it is
- **Witnesses by law.** No charter, council act, county grant or great oath is valid without a Senven witness and seal. Station charters, leases of the underground right, Order bills paid by the treasury: all of it passes under an elven eye.
- **It witnessed the founding.** Senven witnessed the founding letter of the union ([[The Founding of the Levont]]). Its elders are the only people alive who saw the old countries sign, and the only ones who know what the letter really said.
- **A council seat.** It sits as one of the great institutions on the council ([[Government of the Levont]]). Its power isn't votes: it's that nothing is lawful until it has watched.

## Why it matters
- **A quiet veto.** A Senven witness who is "unavailable" can stall a charter for months.
- **The founding letter.** If the official founding story is flattering ([[The Founding of the Levont#Current lean]]), Senven knows how. It has never said so. Everyone wonders why.
- **The fallen kingdom still rules on paper.** The [[Aelnor]] lost their kingdom, but every human law in the Levont still needs their signature. It's the glory-and-decline theme for elves ([[Worldbuilding Sage Lessons#Peoples]]).

## Suggestions (not canon)
- **Inside the house:** restorers who want the old kingdom back, and who sign everything in full Elvish forms, against assimilators who sign in the common tongue. Which way a witness signs is a statement ([[Languages and Naming#Melting-pot rules]]).
- **Shadow:** a witness can be bought, or can "remember" differently. A forged founding letter would need a Senven seal to be believed.
- **Rivals:** the [[Order of the Rising Sun]], which claims founder status Senven has never confirmed. [[Lurian]]'s archive houses, who keep the records but not the right to witness.
- **Believable side:** to its members it is a sacred duty. Someone must remember truly, in a land of short lives and convenient stories.
