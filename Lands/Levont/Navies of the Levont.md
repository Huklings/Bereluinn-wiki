# Navies of the Levont

Who guards the Levont's coasts. Needed because the [[Toad People]] clash with Levont forces again and again.

## What we know (26 Oct)
- **To design:** some counties have navies of their own, and there is a standing fleet at the capital.
- **Considering:** the fleet's port sits **north of the capital**. Going around takes time, which makes the toads a larger threat.
- **Channels could also work**, cutting the trip short ([[The Channels]]).

## What follows from the map
The sea route from the estuary to the capital runs all the way around the Levont's eastern tip: about 1,300 km, or 9 to 13 days of sailing ([[Rivers of the Levont]]). So a toad raid on the south coast has well over a week before the fleet can arrive. Until it does, the county navies hold the line alone.

## Suggestions, not canon
- **The counties get leverage.** If the county navies always hold the line first, they can ask for money, rights or seats in return. The council depends on them, not the other way round. Same friction as [[The March]].
- **A county naval base in slot A.** The sheltered southwest harbours were already suggested for a naval base or shipbuilding ([[Levont Area Map]]).
- **Who has a navy?** The coastal counties are the lush coast (1), the rocky coast (2) and the marshes (4) ([[Counties of the Levont]]). The rocky coast's former raiders are sworn to peace, and only their Land Wardens carry real weapons. Would they keep a navy, and does a ship count as a weapon under the vow?
- **The marshes know boats** from their delta, and their crows carry messages faster than any boat.
- **The north sea is watched already** by the Order's falconers along the rocky coast ([[Suncourt]]).

## Open questions
- Name the fleet's port. Is it the capital's own bay, or a separate harbour town?
- Who commands the standing fleet: the council, a regent, or an admiral with a seat?
- Which counties keep navies, and who pays for them?
- How far can the fleet cut the trip through the channels, and who controls their banks?
