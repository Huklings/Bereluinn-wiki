# The Veil

The wall between the mortal realm and [[The Tapestries]]. Where the veil grows thin, a tapestry's power seeps into the area and changes what lives there.

## What we know
- The veil separates the tapestry realms from the mortal realm.
- In some places it grows thin, and the nearby tapestry's power affects the area.
- **Example:** deep in a forest, the veil to a bestial or nature realm has worn thin. The animals there mutate and grow into dire bears and similar creatures.
- **Time inside a realm mutates you.** Spending time in a tapestry changes a being, body and soul. [[Angels]] are souls that have been changed this way.
- The gods themselves may not cross into the mortal realm. Their power reaches it only through clerics and through thin places in the veil ([[The Tapestries]]).

## You don't see it, you notice it
A thin veil is invisible. There is no shimmering portal or glowing tear. People notice it only through its effects: the area slowly takes on the nature of the realm behind it.
- **Fire realm:** forest fires break out more often, and fires burn brighter.
- **Bestial or nature realm:** animals mutate and grow into dire bears and similar beasts.

## Signs by realm

## How crossings work (decided 26 Oct)
- **Many beings can cross a seam if it is open enough.** That is how dire wolves and other monsters sometimes come over into the mortal realm.
- **A seam open long enough mutates the mortal realm directly.** The tapestry's nature soaks into the land around it.
- **Giants can feel seams, and may even be able to create them.** That is how they step between realms. Whether it comes from their tie to magic is still open.
- So the Martyr's wall is not airtight. It keeps the gods out, but it wears thin, and what is open enough can be crossed.
- *Note on words:* "seam" above means any crossing that has opened, not only the fixed crossing points in the table below. Whether to keep that distinction is open.

## Words for crossings (26 Oct)
A small vocabulary built on the tapestry metaphor, picked so that crossings aren't confused with the Veiled Frontier:

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Seam** | A fixed crossing point. The sacred peaks the [[Giants]] guard, which the dwarves dug into |
| **Weft** | The fabric between realms itself, what you slip through |
| **Thin threads** | Places where the weft is weak and things leak through, like dire beasts near [[The Beast Tapestry]] |
| **Tears** | Damaged or forced crossings. Dangerous, and possibly the work of toad alchemy ([[Toad People]]) |
| **Seamwalkers** | A title for giants in some cultures |

**A clash to decide.** This note already uses "the veil" for the wall between realms, and "a thin veil" is canon. The weft is the same thing under a new name, and "thin threads" is the same as a thin veil. Two ways out:
1. **Keep "the veil"** as the everyday Levont word, and make "the weft" and "seams" the scholars' and giants' words for the same fabric. Different people naming one thing differently is realistic.
2. **Rename the wall to the weft** everywhere. That also lowers the Dragon Age overlap in [[Borrowed Ideas]], where the shared name "Veil" is part of the score (Name 3 to about 0, so 15 to about 12).

## Thin spots drift (decided 25 Oct)
Thin spots don't stay put. Over decades a thin place can heal while a new one opens nearby. The law hasn't kept up: preserve borders were fixed when each preserve was set aside, so some guard healthy land while new thin spots open under villages ([[Preserves and Parks#The borders don't follow the veil (decided 25 Oct)]]).

## What thins the veil
- **Mostly nature.** Thinning is a natural thing. The veil wears thin on its own.
- **Moon nights (your idea, 26 Oct).** On nights when the largest piece of the broken moon is visible, the veil thins heavily across the lands. The moon is [[The Martyr]]'s, and her shattering is what walled the tapestries off.
- **Dark rituals** (canon). Cultists, often of [[The Gods of Desirenthropy]], perform foul rituals to thin the veil in one place on purpose, so that **beings from a tapestry realm can come through in person**. This is incredibly dangerous.

### Working idea, not settled
- The veil thins **by itself** over time.
- **Magic** may thin it too. Magic is connected to the realms, so pulling on fire draws on a fire realm and thins the veil to that realm where the spell is cast. Heavy magic use is then not only dangerous to the caster but ill-advised for everyone nearby.
- **[[Moon Shards]]** do not thin it by themselves; they are stable and clean. Some uses of them may be dangerous.

If this holds:
- **Cities and battlefields** where much magic was used would be thin places. Elden ruins and old battlefields of [[The Sundering]] would be among the thinnest.
- **The gods' limits on [[Magic]]** gain a second reason: weaker magic protects the veil. That strengthens the winners' case in [[Sides of the Sundering]].
