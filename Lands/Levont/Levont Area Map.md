# Levont Area Map

Where each area of the [[Levont]] sits on the map, plus empty **slots** for new ones. Worked out on 22 October 2026 from the first map draft. **The placements are suggestions until you mark them canon.** When a new idea comes up, say which slot it goes in.

![[Levont area slots.png]]

Distances and travel times from the capital are in [[World Scale]], whose scale is canon.

These are the six areas from [[areas of the Levont]], plus the heartland and the March, and three more areas added 25 Oct from old drafts (Merelon, now B; G; D).

## Areas and counties
The Levont has **six counties** ([[Counties of the Levont]]), and each began as one of the six areas. Now there are more places than counties. Either the Levont gets more counties, or a county holds several areas, which makes the area map and the county map two separate layers. Not decided.

**Placed for now (25 Oct, can be rearranged):** the Ackerdean belongs to County 1 (the lush coast), Merelon is a chartered town under the council, and Suncourt is Order land outside the counties. See [[Counties of the Levont#Areas beyond the first six (placed 25 Oct, can be rearranged)]].

## How to use this
- **New idea:** say "this goes in slot G" and it gets added here and to the place's own note.
- **When the map is redrawn:** the slots follow the land, not the pixels. If a coastline moves, the slot moves with it.
- **Interactive map later:** each slot becomes a marker in the Leaflet plugin, linking to its note.
