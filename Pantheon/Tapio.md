# Tapio

God followed by the [[Green Wardens]]. The lore so far describes Tapio's followers, not the god.

## Decided (31 Oct)
- **[[The Beast Tapestry]] is Tapio's.** The wilds, nature, change and chaos: his power as it was when his mantle was made.
- **Tapio and [[Lady Bluecloak]] (Mielikki) were once a married wizard couple.** "Once": they are not together now. Answers 13.2. **Why they parted:** Tapio was fine with the mantle ritual; Lady Bluecloak was neutral, but opposed it at first. Now each is in their own domain, and they cannot meet as often as they would like.
- **His clergy worship him, but stop his tapestry from spilling over,** keeping the balance. The [[Green Wardens]] hunt the dire beasts and mutations that come through from their own god's realm.
- **Domains (31 Oct):** Nature, Change, Freedom, Travel. Alternates: Might, Swarm, Family. Nature and Change are his tapestry itself; Freedom is the unbound wild; Travel is pathfinding and finding the lost.
- **A member of [[Side Pantheons#The nature gods' circle (decided 31 Oct)|the Wild Circle]].**

## What we know
- **Public services:** blessings for duty, overcoming challenges and finding something.
- **Church:** chapter houses hold the clerics, alongside Wardens and druidic circles.
- In Finnish myth, Tapio is a forest god and husband of Mielikki ([[Lady Bluecloak]]).
