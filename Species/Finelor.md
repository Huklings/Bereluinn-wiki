# Finelor

Secluded elves, descended from those who defected to the good side in [[The Sundering]]. Tall, horned and long-fingered, and stigmatized for it.

## What we know
- **The name** is Elvish: *fin* "fair, white, bright" + *nor* "folk", the pale folk. Two n's never meet in Elvish, so *fin-nor* becomes *Finelor* ([[Languages and Naming#Elvish (direction 19 Oct, kit approved 22 Oct)]]).
- Height 230 to 240 cm, with horns and long fingers.
- They underwent a sacrifice and an ordeal that only a few know about.
- The Moon goddess of the [[Elven Pantheon]] is their patron. **Decided 26 Oct:** the Moon goddess was [[The Martyr]], whom the elden people already called a god. She is gone, but her half-working tapestry still lets some powers through.
- Live in seclusion.
- **They first worshipped [[Faelath]], the hunter** of the [[Elven Pantheon]], then left her to join the orderly side (29 Oct). Like all elves they still worship the hunter, warily.
