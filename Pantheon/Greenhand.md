# Greenhand

Neutral goddess of Cultivation ("CG" for short), maker and main god of the [[Halffolk]], the Wendfolk. Created 29 Oct 2026.

## Decided (29 Oct)
- **"Greenhand" is the name she goes by.** No other name is needed.
- **She made the Wendfolk.** She looked on this small people and saw a promising future, something to *cultivate*.
- **The luck was her gift.** During the great war she protected them the way a gardener starts plants in a pot indoors before sowing them out in the wild, granting them nourishment and "luck" to survive. The luck simply remains, grown into them. It draws on her tapestry.
- **Neutral in the Sundering,** but she went along with the plan for the mantle and took one ([[Sides of the Sundering]], [[The Apotheosis]]).
- **[[Demerde]]**, the Wendfolk's hearth patron, is a minor god living in her tapestry.
- The Tonfolk pray more to [[Verdan Ironoath]] than to her.

## Domains (decided 29 Oct)
Pathfinder 2e domains. **Nature** is left out on purpose: [[Lady Bluecloak]], [[Tapio]] and [[The Bud and the Sprout]] already crowd it, and Greenhand is about tending, not the wild.
- **Main:** Protection (the pot, sheltering what's young), Toil (cultivation is daily work), Time (patience and seasons), Earth (the soil).
- **Alternates:** Healing (nourishment), Family (may suit [[Demerde]] better).
- **Luck is a specialization, not a main domain.** It is tied to the Wendfolk only ([[Halffolk]]).

## Her tapestry: the Long Season (decided 29 Oct) Like every tapestry, it embodies its essence rather than containing it: a realm where tending is everywhere and stronger ([[The Tapestries]]).
- **The feel:** a sheltered, endlessly tended country. Deep dark soil, walls and glass far off, and a slow sky where one season lasts for years.
- **Rule:** whatever is tended grows toward what it's meant to become. Whatever is left untended doesn't die; it goes still and waits.
- **Image:** a lit farmhouse at the heart of it all: [[Demerde]]'s hearth.
- **Who lives there:** Demerde, and the souls of Wendfolk who keep tending.

### Near her thin spots ([[The Veil]])
- **No sleeplessness here** (29 Oct): not every tapestry should echo the lands' sleeplessness. A thin spot shows the realm's own rule instead.
- **Growth (your idea, 29 Oct):** things grow larger, or more of them. Beasts and people who give birth near her thin spots may suddenly have twice as many offspring.

### Suggestions (not canon)
- **Weeds and pests grow too.** The thin spot doesn't care what it feeds, so farmers fight double the rats as well as harvest double the grain.
- **Twins as a sign.** Twins born near a thin spot are taken as Greenhand's blessing, or her claim.
- **Does Wendmark sit on her thin spots?** That would explain why so much food flows out of it.
- **A long-open seam** would change the land itself ([[The Veil#How crossings work (decided 26 Oct)]]): a valley where everything is overgrown and oversized.
