# Preserves and Parks

Wilds, preserves and parks across the [[Levont]], patrolled by the [[Green Wardens]].

## Suggestion (not settled)
They sit over scarred land: containment sites for the elemental energies [[Lady Bluecloak]] held, or places where the Moon Shard Storm fell ([[The Moon's Shattering]]). That gives the Wardens a purpose beyond tidying forests.

## The borders don't follow the veil (decided 25 Oct)
Thin spots drift over decades, but each preserve's borders were fixed in law when it was set aside. So some preserves are in the wrong places now:
- **Stale preserves** guard forest where the veil has healed. Nobles and farmers want the land back, and the [[Green Wardens]] are guarding trees.
- **New thin spots** open under villages, fields and roads that no law protects. Moving a village or fencing a new preserve needs the council, the landowner and the count to agree, and none of them want to pay.
- **The old maps** of where the veil was thin, a century ago and more, no longer match the ground. Whoever keeps the current ones (the Wardens? [[Lurian]]'s archives?) knows which preserves are a fiction.
