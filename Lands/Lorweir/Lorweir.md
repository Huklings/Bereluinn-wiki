# Lorweir

A continent bordering the [[Levont]], joined to it through a larger stretch of land. Pictured as steppe, in the spirit of the Mongol steppes. Some notes from October called it "Talweir" by mistake. Talweir is now a spare name ([[Languages and Naming#Spare names]]).

## What we know
- **Rulers:** a large hobgoblin horde, with smaller orc and goblin clans. All of them still need designing.
- **Humans:** some human factions near Lorweir speak the orcish tongue ([[Languages and Naming]]).
- **The old dwarf empire:** it once spanned the whole Levont and Lorweir, with a hold in each large mountain chain, perhaps linked by paths and tunnels under the mountains. You noted this idea is high on the "copy" scale ([[Borrowed Ideas]]). Dwarves would call it *Ankhor-Durm*, "the Stone Realm".
- **Fallen holds:** some old holds are broken and derelict, and dangerous monsters live in them now. Clans that escaped to the Levont may want to take their homes back. See [[Dwarves]].

## Orcs are opponents (decided 25 Oct)
The hobgoblins, orcs and goblins of Lorweir are **opponents**, in the spirit of Tolkien's orcs, not a relatable people waiting to be understood. There are no defector clans with council seats. The Levont fears them for good reason.

## How the horde fits together (decided 31 Oct)
- **The hobgoblins lead.** They are the horde's nobles.
- **The orcs are brutes** who roam in tribes, far less clever. Sometimes they fight the hobgoblins, sometimes not.
- **The goblins** swarm, steal and multiply.
- **Their gods** are hard-to-redeem gods of desirenthropy, each the maker of one people ([[The Gods of Desirenthropy#The horde's gods (decided 31 Oct)]]): the Bloodfather (hobgoblins), the Many-Mouthed (goblins), and the Bloodhawk (orcs).

## The khan is dead (decided 25 Oct)
The horde's old khan has just died. Steppe empires stall at a succession: the Mongols turned back from Europe in 1242 when the Great Khan Ögedei died, because every commander rode home to choose the next one.
- **Two ways it can go:** the horde splits into rival heirs, or a new khan needs a war to prove himself and turns west.
- **For the Levont:** the union was founded against the horde, and the council keeps the fear alive ([[Government of the Levont]]). Now the threat may come true, or vanish.
- It is one of the pressures behind **why now** ([[Timeline]]).

Still open: the old khan's name, the heirs, and how long a horde succession takes.
