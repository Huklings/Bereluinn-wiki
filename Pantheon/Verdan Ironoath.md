# Verdan Ironoath

God of oaths, loyalty and guardianship. Listed as neutral, but reads as lawful.

## What we know
- **Domains (draft):** Loyalty, Resolve and Guardianship may be homebrew (9.15).
- **Imagery:** granite statue with an oath tablet.
- [[Dwarves]] keep shrines to him as the god of duty.

## Keeper of every oath (decided 2 Nov)
- **Verdan Ironoath writes down and keeps every oath,** including the oaths that bind a person to a god.
- That makes him a god even the "evil" gods want to talk to: every claim on a soul, and every promise between gods, sits in his keeping. Gods talk with gods at a distance; he still never deals with mortals in person.
- **He decides which oaths bind.** An oath he judges binding *dunks* the soul in that god's tapestry: far more claimed than worship alone would make it, and often with power granted by the god. An oath binds only once the god has answered with some aid, usually power from its domain (domain spells, boons, strength). Even a desperate battlefield prayer ("help me win, and I will worship") can count ([[Death and the Afterlife#How a soul finds its god (decided 2 Nov)]]).

### What follows (suggestions, not canon)
- **Folklore:** "Ironoath heard that." Said when someone makes a careless promise; children are told every promise lands in his book.
- **Hooks:** a desirenthropy cult tries to learn what is written in his book; a forged oath turns up in a lord's chapel; a god disputes a soul and Ironoath's record is the only witness.
