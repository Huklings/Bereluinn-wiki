# The Founding of the Levont

The union of several former countries into the [[Levont]].

## What we know
- **About 120 years ago** (decided 22 Oct). Just past living memory: nobody alive remembers the old countries, but grandparents told stories. See [[Timeline]].
- **Under pressure from the horde** of [[Lorweir]] (decided 22 Oct, with the timeline). No old country could face it alone. See [[World Scale]] for how the numbers compare.
- **A strong regency** followed for about 40 years (120 to 80 years ago). Its bailiffs and laws are the system everyone lives under now.
- One of the old countries **brokered the union**, offering a larger country to unify and defend them all. Its old seat is now the capital ([[Government of the Levont]]).
- At the founding, the land was redrawn into six counties that cut across the old borders ([[Counties of the Levont]]).

## Current lean
A treaty of necessity, with a flattering official story that some regions quietly dispute. This is the central tension between the council and the regions.
