# Pantheon

The gods of the Levont. Every god was once a mortal wizard-lord. The good gods ascended at the end of [[The Sundering]] through the mantle ritual their own side invented ([[The Apotheosis]]). The idea is inspired by Age of Wonders, where the rulers are wizards.

Taking a mantle created each god's realm, a tapestry, and they withdrew into it, because a lord loose on the mortal plane was dangerous for everyone else. See [[The Tapestries]].

Each god's power is stored in an essence. When a god dies, someone else can take up the mantle, so anyone can ascend. See [[Divine Essences]].

No god may step into the mortal realm. Gods act through the powers they grant their clerics and through thin places in [[The Veil]]. Souls that spend long in a god's realm become [[Angels]]. Every god, the neutral ones included, began as a wizard-lord (below).

[[The Martyr]] died in the Moonshard Calamity during [[The Sundering]], by her own choice, before her mantle fully materialised. Her shattering broke a moon and walled the tapestries off from the mortal realm ([[The Moon's Shattering]]).

"Good" is the winners' name. The war was a split among wizards over the tapestry plan ([[Sides of the Sundering]]).

## Where the gods came from (decided 26 Oct)
- **In the Elden Era there were no gods, only wizard-lords** with uncontained power. Ordinary people already called them gods, because their power rivalled a god's.
- **Every race was made by a wizard-lord,** who infused it with their magic. That is where each race's looks come from. [[Ishgadrum]] made the [[Dwarves]]; the elves had a maker of their own.
- **The lords were destroying the land** for their creations, and threatening the moons.
- **The mantle ritual came from the side that wanted to rein that power in.** It bound the loose magic to each lord as a divine mantle ("mantle" is figurative) and created their tapestry. Holding a mantle now gives domain over a tapestry. Divinity lets a lord live essentially forever, but they can still be killed. This was the tapestry plan at the heart of [[The Sundering]].
- **Each tapestry is permeated by its lord's power as it was when it was made.** That is why tapestries are hard to pin down. [[The Beast Tapestry]] is not only beasts, but the wilds, nature, change and chaos.
- **[[The Martyr]] was making her mantle and died before it fully materialised.** Her death broke a moon, the only moon that has ever broken, and left the [[Moon Shards]]. Her shattering also walled the tapestries off from the mortal realm. Her own tapestry exists but doesn't fully work.
- **A maker is not the only god a people has (29 Oct).** Each race has one maker, but a people can call several gods its own, and a god can be dear to several peoples.
- **Minor gods (your idea, 29 Oct).** Six hundred years after the Sundering, beings, monsters and creatures living inside the tapestries have grown strong enough to act as minor gods. They hold no mantle, and some may answer prayers. Compare [[Angels]], souls changed by a tapestry.
- The winners took the mantles and became the [[The Lawful Accords|Accords]]. The strongest losers took mantles anyway and had to accept the same terms ([[The Gods of Desirenthropy]]).

## The Lawful Accords (the "good" gods)
The winners of the Sundering. See [[The Lawful Accords]].
- [[The Sunturner]]: healing, sun, ambition. Patron of the [[Order of the Rising Sun]].
- [[The Martyr]]: sacrifice and protection. The Martyr's myth is the binding of the warring gods.

## Neutral gods
- [[Lusitri]]: art, beauty, transformation, "endowment".
- [[Lady Bluecloak]]: nature and animals. She contained the elemental energies.
- [[The Smiling Father]]: meets the dead. Fate.
- [[Verdan Ironoath]]: oaths, loyalty, guardianship. May be lawful rather than neutral (9.6).
- [[Lurian]]: secrecy and archives.
- [[The Bud and the Sprout]]: newborns, orchards, new-body resurrection.
- [[Greenhand]]: goddess of Cultivation, maker of the [[Halffolk]]. Neutral in the Sundering, but went along with the mantle plan (29 Oct).

## The gods of desirenthropy
- [[The Gods of Desirenthropy]]: the losers of the Sundering, who ascended anyway. Bound by the terms to tapestries of their own, still pursuing domination, greed and their own agendas. A loose name, not a united front. The Levont knows them mostly as bogeymen.

## Minor gods
Beings grown strong inside the tapestries since the Sundering, without a mantle (your idea, 29 Oct).
- [[Demerde]]: an "it", the hearth patron of the [[Halffolk]], living in [[Greenhand]]'s tapestry.

## Not yet placed
- [[Tapio]]: god of the [[Green Wardens]], owner of [[The Beast Tapestry]], once married to [[Lady Bluecloak]] (31 Oct). Group unknown (13.1).
- [[Ishgadrum]]: maker of the [[Dwarves]], an elden wizard-lord. Which side he took is open.
- [[Elven Pantheon]]: [[Athréth]] the maker, [[Gaesín]] (knowledge and wisdom), [[Faelath]] the hunter, [[Ádhín]] the goddess of luck, and the Moon goddess (the Martyr). Elves also favour Tapio and Lady Bluecloak (29 Oct).

## Side pantheons
Gods also form smaller groups around a shared goal, across good and evil, and one god can belong to several. Mortals can worship a whole group. See [[Side Pantheons]].
