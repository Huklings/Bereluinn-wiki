# Lustiri

Neutral god of art, beauty, transformation and "endowment".

## What we know
- **Domains (draft):** five, including Soul, which can speak to the dead. Five is a lot (9.8).
- **Endowment (defined 29 Oct):** the power to grant someone power, bestowing gifts and talents upon others.
- **Spelling:** **Lustiri** is canon (19 Oct). "Lusitri" was a typo; the file name still uses it until it's renamed in Obsidian.
- [[Dwarves]] keep shrines to Lustiri as the god of beauty.

## Flavor budget suggestion
Limit art, beauty and transformation imagery to theatres, craft guilds and certain households (9.16).
