# The Moon's Shattering

Also called **the Moonshard Calamity**. It happened during [[The Sundering]]: [[The Martyr]] chose to die, and her unfinished mantle shattered and walled [[The Tapestries]] off from the mortal realm. A moon broke with her.

**Decided 26 Oct:** she was making her mantle and died before it fully materialised, and that is what broke the moon. It is the only moon that has ever broken. The elden people already called her the moon goddess.

**Moon nights (your idea, 26 Oct):** on nights when the largest piece of the broken moon is visible, the veil thins heavily across the lands ([[The Veil]]).

The world once had three moons. One of them broke, and **two remain** (decided 29 Oct). The broken one left a belt of debris around the planet, like the rings of real planets. Stones from the belt still fall now and then as [[Moon Shards]], small rocks full of power that fuel magic and the [[The Railroad|railroad]].

The Moon Shard Storm is tied to it, and it may be linked to the fall of the [[Aelnor]] kingdom.
