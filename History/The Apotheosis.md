# The Apotheosis

The ascension of mortal wizard-lords past the mortal coil, to become the good gods. It was a **ritual**, built on the discoveries of the old wizards.

**Decided 26 Oct:** this is the mantle ritual. The side that wanted to rein in the lords invented it. It bound the world's loose magic to each lord as a mantle and created their tapestry. Before it, the elden lords held uncontained power and ordinary people already called them gods ([[Pantheon#Where the gods came from (decided 26 Oct)]]).

## Who knows the truth
The other gods all witnessed it. Perhaps some very old elves and dragons did too, but they now live with the gods in [[The Tapestries]]. **No one in the mortal realm truly knows what happened.** Like many real-world myths, the tale has been told and retold, and warped by word of mouth.

Afterward, each god wove a realm of their own, a tapestry, and withdrew into it, because staying on the mortal plane was dangerous for everyone else. See [[The Tapestries]].
