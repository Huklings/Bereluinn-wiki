# The Railroad

Trains cross the [[Levont]], powered by [[Moon Shards]].

## What we know
- **The first line opened about 50 years ago** (decided 22 Oct, see [[Timeline]]). Old people remember the first train.
- The trains run on moon shards.
- **It's the Levont's fastest way to answer an attack.** Without it, a full army takes 4 to 8 weeks to reach the March. See [[World Scale]].
- Walking across the Levont takes about four weeks; the train takes about two days ([[World Scale]]).
- Area 3's salt steppe is mostly seen from a train window. Villages there host taverns along the railroad ([[areas of the Levont]]).

## Station charters (decided 25 Oct)
Every station is a grant from the council. A lord or town with a station gets rich: trade, travellers, taverns, and a say in what the trains carry.
- **The council's patronage machine.** Where a line runs and where it stops is how the council rewards loyalists. Every railroad map is a map of favours ([[Government of the Levont]]).
- **Grudges along the line.** A town the line passed by, or a lord whose station went to a rival, has a reason to hate the council.

Still open: who signs a charter, and can a county refuse a line through its land?

## Connections
- [[Moon Shards]]: its fuel.
- [[Order of the Rising Sun]], the council: who controls the lines matters to both.

## Open questions
- Who built it, who owns it, and who runs it: the council, a company, a guild, the Order? (14.7)
- Did the first line run from the capital to the estuary? The capital can't be fed by river, and the sea route round the east takes 9 to 13 days ([[Rivers of the Levont]]).
- Where do the lines run, and which regions have no railroad?
- Who supplies the shards, and what happens to the trains in a shortage?
- Shards are stable and clean, so the trains do not thin [[The Veil]]. Could a misuse of shards on the railroad still be dangerous?
- Does it carry soldiers? What did it change about war and the old war machine? (1.3)
