# Wendmark

The country of the Wendfolks ([[Halffolk]]): a region of farms, shires, a few small towns and cities. Few outsiders ever visit, but food floods out of it in trade. Named 28 Oct 2026.

## The name
Old English *wendan*, "to turn, to wander", plus *mearc*, "border land". The land where the roads turn away.

## Decided (28 Oct)
- **Its own region,** with a few small towns and cities.
- **Few visit; food flows out.** Its farms feed much of the Levont through trade.
- **People miss the paths.** The halffolk's luck works on the whole people: travellers take a wrong fork, and end up back where they started.
- **Guides.** Wendfolk guides are how outsiders get in.

## Suggestions (not canon)
- **The roads wend away.** The fog comes in, a fork looks wrong, and you're back at the market town. Maps of the area never quite agree.
- **A ring of border market towns** where outsiders and Wendfolks meet and trade. Tonfolk traders from the cities, carters and brewing contests all gather there.
- **Kin-tokens.** A guide, or a token from a Wendfolk family, leads you in. Who holds a token is a plot hook in itself.
- **Luck pools** may be what makes the roads wend ([[Halffolk#Suggestions (not canon)]]). Then a famine is when the roads open.
- **Who can't find it:** tax collectors and quartermasters. In wartime the council may depend on Wendfolk goodwill for grain.

## Where
**Slot E, the northeast** (**canon, 28 Oct**; [[Levont Area Map]]). About 336 km from the capital, between the marshes and the eastern range.

- **Both lands grow.** [[The Ackerdean]] is the farm country of the west, and Wendmark the farm country of the east (decided 28 Oct). The Levont has two larders, on opposite sides.
- **Far from the capital,** which suits a land few visit.
- **Neighbours:** the marshes (Area 4) to the west, the charming valley (Area 5) to the south, Merelon (slot C) to the east.
- **A northern coast,** far from the toads' raids in the south. Its food can leave by sea, or down the rivers through the marshes.
