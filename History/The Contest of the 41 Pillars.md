# The Contest of the 41 Pillars

A masonry contest between the sun elf [[Heleriam Aeloven]] and the dwarf [[Bosurum Veingold]], set "after the sundering". It ended in a fight.

## What we know
- Heleriam built 41 pillars. They showed "various stigmas" (wording to check, 12.10).
- Something in the 41st pillar provoked Bosurum and his guards to draw arms at a banquet.
- Bosurum demanded to test Heleriam's skill in smithing.
- Heleriam was killed. His son avenged him.
- The surviving account favors the elves.
