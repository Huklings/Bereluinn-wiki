# Sides of the Sundering

The war was not good against evil. At its heart was a split among the wizards over the tapestry plan, and the other side was a mix of several groups. "The good gods" is the winners' name for themselves.

## The divide
The two sides are known as **[[The Lawful Accords]]** and **[[The Gods of Desirenthropy]]**.
- **For the tapestries:** the wizard-lords who won. They ascended, wove [[The Tapestries]], withdrew, and limited [[Magic]] and ascension. They became the good gods of the [[Pantheon]].
- **Against the tapestries:** wizards who saw the plan as a bad idea. It limited their own power. Worse, it took power away from people who had not even reached it yet, which made it unfair to everyone who came after.

## The other side was a mix
- Wizards opposed to the tapestry plan.
- Possibly the [[Dragons]], who had enslaved the [[Dwarves]].
- [[The Gods of Desirenthropy]], whose tapestries of domination and greed are tied to the spirit in Area 5 ([[areas of the Levont]]).
- Others not yet named.

## Each side's case
| | For the tapestries | Against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Their argument** | Lords on the mortal plane ravage the land for their creations, threaten the moons and kill each other. Power must be limited for the world to survive. | The limits lock the door behind those already through it. Mortals not yet born lose a power that was theirs to reach. |
| **What the other side says about them** | They pulled up the ladder and called it mercy. | They would risk the world for their own power. |

If magic thins [[The Veil]] (a working idea), the tapestry side has a second argument: unlimited magic for everyone would wear the veil thin everywhere.

Both cases are true enough to believe. That is what keeps the war from being black and white.

## The elves split three ways
- [[Aelnor]]: fought beside the good gods.
- [[Finelor]]: defected from the betrayers to the good side.
- [[Northern Elves]]: stayed with the betrayer gods. Their remnants live on the northern continent and seek undeath.

## Suggestions (not canon)
- **The [[Finelor]]** defected from the opposing side to the tapestry side. Their secret ordeal could be the price of changing sides.
- **The neutral gods** could be wizards who stayed out of the split, joined late, or struck a compromise.
- **The opposing cause outlived the war.** Today's heirs would want magic restored and the limits broken. They might be villains, revolutionaries or sympathetic rebels, and [[Moon Shards]] would be their best tool.
- **The losers' history** would be a suppressed history, the kind [[Lurian]]'s clergy might keep (9.13).
