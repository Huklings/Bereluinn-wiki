
Area 1: A lush coast, an area of of ruined temples, filled with forgotten secrets and treasure, but one can often find warbands of roaming soldiers and bandits in the area, perhaps of fallen beings influencing them, or from whatever items they've found in these dark depths. The area in itself and its culture if welcoming, warm and town within its region hosts the countries largest Tinkering Fair. The Local nobles are keen on presenting out battle honours in this area and with the roaming bands, sometimes being backed by local lords... holdings are often changing hands.

Area 2: A rocky coast, who's people once lived and thrived from pillaging and violence, now have vowed to put away their blades, turning swords to ploughshares into this carved area. The only ones allowed to wield anything other than simple weapons for self defence are the units of Land Wardens and their hunting dogs used to ferry out wayward troublemakers and monsters.

Area 3: steppe, before the steppe became what it now is, it was a lush forest and mountainous little valley. the war of Elden eras having salted the earth. here works miners, and salt makers in great camps. The salt itself when mixed can produce strange effects and some alchemists use it to create Fire Dust, which people swear allows you to speak to Fiery beings. most of the time you only pass through herese on trains and the camps are dreary places. The few villages that exist here host taverns along the railroad and the paths for wayward travelers.


area 4: marshes, the deep marshers once held elden elven cities, large and glamourous. Now they host Levont cities, mostly containing the forest loving elves, humans and halffolk. The folk of the marshes are known to tame and breed Red winged crows native to the lands, these are kept as playmates and have proven to be expert companions for captains and their regiments, for the red-winged birds house an far larger intelligence than their more common counter parts, able to mimic noises and be taught further tricks. However within the Marshes somewhere rests a strange old vault of information and tomes. The people outside of the marshes insists that the vault is actually in the marshes county seat, where their captains serve and practise under a religious order of bird worshippers.

area 5: a charming valley in this seemingly charming valley rests a restless spirit of a grandious and devious being locked in the remnants of a Elden dwarven hold. The spirits influence is connected to the tapestries of domination and greed, as well as the primal tapestries. Those who live to close to the old dwarven ruins can sometimes feel the effects whenever the veil becomes too thin. Insomnia and some becomes to influenced by it leading to a self proclaimeed Beast lord and Tamer plagueing the land. This is also unbeknownst to many where one of the more famous guilds of thieves rests right under neath the local lords Garrison.

Area 6: plains  this area iso ne of the largest openings into the Lovant, guarded by several garrisoned Way Stations to guard from the roaving terrors from the mountains and the forests that borders this county of plains. the county Seat hosts an bi annuall military parade, where each town and their garrison must present their own parts of the parade itself. Each town also has their own Village constables and specialist soldiers. Also located within the County Seat, which is built on an Elden Human city has managed ti unearth the ancient religious forge, some Runic weapons sometimes join the markets.


Area 7: Merelon, a mountainous rocky coast where the elven city of magic once stood. Now the Larhouse academy of magic, with the old standing stones passed off as rubble, cut off by the foggy Calenwood and its druids. See [[Merelon]].

Area 8: Suncourt, the Order of the Rising Sun's home behind the northern marches: the Tribunal, the festival town of Gildhem, the falconers of the coast watch, and the warped Glasswood to the south. See [[Suncourt]].

Area 9: the Ackerdean, a fertile valley behind the lush coast, with convoys, ruins, the ancient city of Aldbury and its dwarven oath hall, and a drowned spire off the coast. See [[The Ackerdean]].
