# The Channels

Dwarf-cut waterways along the Levont's old elven rivers. The elves were never asked, and they and the dwarves still argue over it.

## What we know (decided 26 Oct)
- **Built by dwarf engineering along the old elven rivers** ([[Rivers of the Levont]]).
- **Built during the war in which the wizards became gods,** which you called the ascension war.
- **The elves were never asked.** It was contempt, not a refusal that was overruled.
- **The elves lay claim to the rivers as their namers.** The dwarves built the channels anyway, during war.
- **A grievance they still argue over.** It fueled conflicts and still does.
- **It lasts because of how the elves pass knowledge down.** No living elf saw it built ([[Death and the Afterlife#Elves are reborn (decided 26 Oct)]]), so the grudge is cultural: taught, sung and handed on.

## Suggestions, not canon
- **A council quarrel that never ends.** Every time repairs, tolls or dredging come up, the elves and dwarves reopen it. Good fuel for the melting-pot opening ([[Government of the Levont]]).
- **Both sides have a fair point.** The elves say the water was theirs before the stone was. The dwarves say they built it, so they hold the rights. Nobody can rule on it cleanly.
- **Repair is politics.** Some channels may be silted up or collapsed. The dwarves want credit and a say, the counties want the tolls, and the council needs them open.
- **Chokepoints.** Whoever holds the banks can charge tolls, slow the fleet or close a channel. That gives the counties another lever over the capital ([[Navies of the Levont]]).
- **Strong souls remember.** An elf who recalls the channels being cut from a past life would be living proof the old stories are true, and a political prize. Nobody can prove such a claim, or disprove it.

## Things to check
- **Who was in charge when they were dug?** The dwarves were enslaved by the [[Dragons]] until they were freed at the end of [[The Sundering]]. If the channels were cut during that war, the dwarves were either still thralls digging on dragon orders, or newly free and fighting. If it was under dragon command, the dwarves *couldn't* have asked, and the elves' grudge is aimed at the wrong people. A twist for later, if anyone ever finds out.
- **The capital and the railroad.** [[Rivers of the Levont]] says the capital can't be fed by water, because the rivers north of the divide run north and the farm rivers run south. That is why the capital lives on the railroad. A working channel across the divide would undo that. Broken or silted channels across the divide keep the railroad vital, and make reopening them a fight worth having.
- **The date** hangs on the timeline question ([[Timeline#The Ascension War (open, 26 Oct)]]).
