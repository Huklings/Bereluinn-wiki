# Masters of the Larhouse

The governing body of the Larhouse, the Levont's licensed academy of magic in [[Merelon]]. Mixed species, with many [[Aelnor]] scholars. Added 25 Oct 2026. **The idea is decided; the name still needs work.**

**Changed 12 Nov:** the name "Larhouse" is dropped, and the school is one of at least two licensed schools, founded by [[Wizard Houses]]. The masters, and both their name and the school's, wait for the naming pass. The candidates below are kept for reference; the Larmoot shares a root with Witan, which you turned down.

## Name candidates
- **The Larmoot:** common tongue *lār* "lore" + *mōt* "assembly", like the Anglo-Saxon *witenagemot*.
- **The Stone Bench:** after the elden standing stones in the Larhouse grounds.
- **An Elvish name** for the older, elven-led faction inside it: *Cloesola*, "stone light" (*cloe* + *sola*).

## What it is
- **It speaks for every licensed caster** in the Levont, through a council seat ([[Government of the Levont]]).
- **It wants fewer limits** on magic, and the churches want more. The gods' limits are the faith's teaching; the masters treat them as a technical problem ([[Magic]]).
- **It controls who may practise.** A licence comes from the Larhouse, and graduates owe the council years of service.

## Suggestions (not canon)
- **Shadow:** Birdsong grows inside the Larhouse, and the masters would rather not know ([[Merelon]]).
- **Rivals:** the churches; the [[Calenwood Circle|Calenwood circle]] (no longer next door since 12 Nov); unlicensed hedge casters.
