# Aelnor

Sun-touched elves. Proud and adventurous. Their kingdom fell, and they now live within the [[Levont]].

## What we know
- **The name** is Elvish: *ael* "sun-warmed, golden" + *nor* "folk", the sun-warmed folk. The *Ael-* prefix marks the sun houses ([[Languages and Naming#Elvish (direction 19 Oct, kit approved 22 Oct)]]).
- **Their names are on the rivers.** The Levont's rivers keep Elvish names from before the human countries, which Aelnor elders still say correctly ([[Rivers of the Levont]]).
- Fought alongside the good gods "centuries ago" in [[The Sundering]].
- Once had a kingdom, with cities in the middle and north.
- Live in a district of the Levont (earlier drafts said "the city").
- Wooden apartments are part of their architecture.
- Sun elves (and half-elves) are now admitted to the [[Order of the Rising Sun]].
- [[Heleriam Aeloven]] worked stone and metal at master level, so they are not only wood builders.
- Worship the [[Elven Pantheon]].
- **[[The Sunturner]] is their warrior goddess** (29 Oct). Elves worship her as one of their gods, but only the Aelnor invoke her in battle, as a more benevolent warrior goddess than [[Faelath]], the elven hunter. They turned to her when Faelath fell out of favour.

## Connections
- [[Senven]]: the Witness House, Aelnor notaries who must witness every great charter in the Levont (25 Oct).
- [[Northern Elves]]: the elves who joined the betrayer gods. Unlike them, other elves can be resurrected.
- [[areas of the Levont]]: Area 4's marshes once held large, glamorous elven cities and are now home to forest-loving elves.
