# The Lawful Accords

The side that won [[The Sundering]]: the wizard-lords who backed the tapestry plan, ascended, and became what most of the Levont calls the good gods. Against them stand [[The Gods of Desirenthropy]].

The name may be one that mortals gave them, and it is only one way of grouping the gods. Its members can also belong to other [[Side Pantheons]].

## What we know
- The **accords** are the terms that ended the war and bind every god: no god may step into the mortal realm. The Lawful Accords are the side that upholds them.
- **They invented the mantle ritual (26 Oct)** to rein in the lords' power. It bound loose magic into mantles and created [[The Tapestries]]. They withdrew from the mortal realm, and limited [[Magic]] and ascension ([[Pantheon#Where the gods came from (decided 26 Oct)]]).
- They could not stop the most powerful opposing wizards from ascending too.
- Their gods include [[The Sunturner]] and [[The Martyr]]. See [[Pantheon]].
