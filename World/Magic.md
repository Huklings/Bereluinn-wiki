# Magic

Magic today is weaker than it was. After they ascended, the gods limited magic, broke it and made it weaker, and they limited the way to godhood. That is why the world isn't full of gods.

## What we know
- The gods themselves were wizards before they ascended ([[The Apotheosis]]).
- They deliberately weakened and broke magic, and restricted how anyone can ascend.
- [[Moon Shards]] can power magic. On their own they are stable and clean: they do not thin the veil. Some uses may be dangerous.
- Each god's power is stored in an essence ([[Divine Essences]]).

## Reading the existing lore through this (suggestions, not canon)
- **Elden relics** come from a time of full-strength magic: runic weapons from the ancient religious forge (Area 6), the old vault of tomes (Area 4), the elden ruins everywhere ([[areas of the Levont]]). They would be rare, valuable and dangerous.
- **Moon shards** may be one of the few ways around the gods' limits. That would make shard use a quiet act of defiance, and the [[The Railroad|railroad]] a political object.
- **[[The Sundering]]**, the whole war and its ending, is when magic was broken. That is why everything "elden" is stronger than anything made today.
- **The war was fought over this.** Wizards who opposed the limits called them unfair to everyone not yet powerful. See [[Sides of the Sundering]].
- **Two readings of the gods.** They weakened magic to protect the world, or to protect their own thrones. Both can be believed in the Levont.

## How magic is restricted (decided 26 Oct)
The gods are the wizards of old, who all ascended, and their ascension is why magic is restricted. The restriction is built into how the world works, the loom and the tapestries, so nobody has to stand guard over it:
- **Magic was spent on the weaving.** Before the ascension, magic ran loose through the loom. The mantle ritual bound it to the lords ([[Pantheon#Where the gods came from (decided 26 Oct)]]). The wizards gathered it up and wove it into their tapestries. Very little loose thread is left in the mortal world, so nearly all power is borrowed from a god's realm, arcane power included.
- **Casting is pulling threads.** A cleric pulls with permission. A wizard pulls without asking, which is a kind of theft. The gods may tolerate small pulls and feel the big ones.
- **Loose magic pools in odd places:** seams, thin places and old sites from before the ascension. That's where mages gather, where [[Giants]] stand guard, and where [[Dragons]], older than the gods' realms, might still draw power the old way.
- **Tears are dangerous.** A tear spills unshaped magic from the gaps between tapestries: powerful and corrupting. It suits toad alchemy ([[Toad People]]).
- **A dying god leaks magic.** If a tapestry unravels, its threads come loose into the world. Suddenly there's free magic again, and the remaining gods would be very nervous.

The gods' will can sit on top of this: churches hunting wizards who pull too much, because the gods remember what happens when mortals gather enough power.

*Suggestion:* [[Moon Shards]] fit this neatly. They are the Martyr's raw power, shattered before it became an essence ([[Divine Essences]]), so they may be the one plentiful source of loose thread. That's why they're clean, and why using them is a quiet way around the gods.

### The magic traditions (agreed 26 Oct)
The Pathfinder 2e traditions become ways of pulling threads, not separate sources:
- **Divine:** pulls with a god's permission.
- **Arcane:** pulls by skill, without asking. A kind of theft.
- **Primal:** draws on loose magic at the seams and old places.
- **Occult:** not yet placed.

You think Pathfinder 2e's full spell lists are far too open. That's for another time, in the PF2e homebrew.

## Where it's taught (25 Oct)
**Changed 12 Nov:** the name "Larhouse" is dropped, and it is not the only licensed school. There are **at least two licensed schools** in the Levont, and more places to learn in the Veiled Frontier that mages know of and ordinary people don't care about. The academies were founded by the [[Wizard Houses]], descended from the wizard-lords' apprentices; before [[The Parting]] there were no schools at all. Anyone can learn, but it is costly, hard and rigorous.

The Merelon school (name to come) is built over an elden elven city whose standing stones still stand in its grounds ([[Merelon]]). Licensed graduates owe the council years of service. This answers part of 14.6.

## Cost (working idea, not settled)
Magic draws on the realms. Pulling on fire may thin [[The Veil]] to a fire realm where the spell is cast. Heavy magic use would then be dangerous and ill-advised, and the gods' limits would protect the veil as well as their thrones.

## Open questions
Sanderson's Second Law: these limits are where the stories come from.
- **How** did the gods break magic? (When: as part of how the Sundering ended.) (Partly answered 26 Oct: they gathered loose magic into their tapestries. See above.)
- What exactly is weaker: range, power, how many can use it, what it can touch?
- What can magic still not do at all?
- **14.6** Who uses magic today, how are they trained, and how are they regarded?
- How did the gods limit ascension? A barrier, a hidden location, a cost, a test?
- Do moon shards or elden relics let someone exceed the limits?
- Does anyone know the gods did this? Is it taught, secret, or heresy?
