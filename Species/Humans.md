# Humans

The most varied people of the Levont, and the only one born with an uncoloured soul. Much is still open; this note gathers what is decided.

## Souls and their makers (decided 2 Nov)
- **Born uncoloured.** Every other people is born coloured by its maker. Humans are born blank, so whatever they worship paints them, and gods compete for their devotion. This is the human "freedom" ([[Death and the Afterlife#How a soul finds its god (decided 2 Nov)]]).
- **Several makers, one choice.** Humans never had a single maker. Several makers made them, which is why humans are so varied, and they chose together to leave human souls uncoloured.
- **Five makers (decided 8 Nov).** Humans had five makers. Two are known: [[The Smiling Father]] and [[The Sunturner]]. The other three are **unknown** for now.

- **Why the soul is blank (decided 8 Nov): too many cooks.** With five hands in the pot, no one maker could fairly leave their mark on humans. Rather than fight over whose colour humans would wear, they left them uncoloured and untouched, to be fair to all five.
- **The first touch.** Within days of a birth, a parent presses the newborn against a shrine or a priest's hand to give it a first colour. A baby who dies blank stays with [[The Smiling Father]] a while, then fades.

### What follows (suggestions, not canon)
- **The other side:** the Accords call the blank soul a gift, since only humans truly choose. Desirenthropy preachers say it proves humans were made to be harvested.
- **Still open:** who the three unknown makers are, and whether any of them are among today's gods.
- **The Sunturner was herself one of the newer humans (decided 8 Nov).** She was made shortly before [[The Sundering]], rose to be a wizard-lord, and joined in the making of humans. In a sense she is the youngest of the gods ([[The Sunturner]]).
- **Hooks:** a midwife sect that secretly first-touches every baby to one god; a noble child left untouched on purpose by a godless father; a dwarf who envies humans their blank start.
