# Ishgadrum

Creator of the [[Dwarves]]. An elden wizard-lord who made them by infusing them with his magic, which gave them their looks, before [[The Sundering]].

## What we know
- Clad in opal armor.
- **A wizard-lord, not an older god (26 Oct).** There were no gods in the Elden Era, only lords whom people called gods ([[Pantheon#Where the gods came from (decided 26 Oct)]]).
- Dwarves keep shrines to him, alongside [[Verdan Ironoath]] and [[Lusitri]].
