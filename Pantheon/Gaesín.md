# Gaesín

The elven god of knowledge and wisdom, in the [[Elven Pantheon]]. Named 29 Oct 2026.

## The name
Elvish, from Gaelic *gaois*, "wisdom", plus *-ín*, "young, beloved": "dear wisdom". Said GAY-sheen ([[Languages and Naming#Elvish (direction 19 Oct, kit approved 22 Oct)]]). No match found in Sindarin or Quenya.

## Decided (29 Oct)
- **God of knowledge and wisdom.**
- **Domains:** Knowledge, Truth, Introspection, Travel. Suggested alternates (not chosen): Creation, Dreams.
- **Not a memory god.** An earlier idea, Cuímel, made this god one of memory. A memory god that judged past lives or kept grudges would bring the elves too close to the dwarves, and leaning on "memory" made the god stiff.

## His tapestry: the Far Road (decided 30 Oct)
Like every tapestry, it embodies its essence rather than containing it: a realm where seeking is everywhere and stronger ([[The Tapestries]]).
- **Rule:** everything can be known, but only by going there. Nothing is told; it has to be walked to.
- **Image:** one road winding through every kind of land, with lantern-lit waystations where pilgrims trade what they've learned, and a horizon that never comes closer.
- **Who lives there:** wanderers who never arrive, and long-legged birds such as cranes.

### Near its thin spots ([[The Veil]])
- **Paths run longer or shorter** than they should.
- **Cairns build themselves.**
- **People blurt out truths.**
- **Lost travellers come back** knowing far-off places.

### His shadow
He never arrives. He can learn anything, but never stops seeking long enough to act on it.
