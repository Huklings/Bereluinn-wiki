# The Smiling Father

Neutral god who meets the dead.

## What we know
- **Domains (partial):** Fate. Under his doctrine, even gods must eventually pass on.
- **Followers:** only those who met him and returned.
- [[Dwarves]], though death-focused, don't keep shrines to him (11.11).

## He walks the dead (decided 2 Nov)
- **He walks every soul** to the tapestry of the god that lays claim to it. He holds the place other settings give a judge of the dead, but he does not judge, and he has no restrictions.
- **Where he walks you** depends on how your soul was painted by worship when you died ([[Death and the Afterlife#How a soul finds its god (decided 2 Nov)]]).
- **He knows at a glance.** He looks upon a soul and knows which god it belongs to, whether it was painted by a lifetime of worship or dunked by a binding oath.
- **Souls with no colour** (your leaning, 2 Nov, to revisit): they stay with him for a while, then fade away permanently. This matters most for humans, who are born uncoloured ([[Humans]]), and for babies who die before their first touch.
