# Demerde

A minor god of the hearth, and an "it". The Wendfolk ([[Halffolk]]) call it their own. Created 29 Oct 2026.

## The name
Demeter, the Greek goddess of grain and harvest, blended with German *Erde*, "earth".

## Decided (29 Oct)
- **A minor god:** a being grown strong inside a tapestry since the Sundering, with no mantle ([[Pantheon#Minor gods]]).
- **It lives in [[Greenhand]]'s tapestry,** at its hearth.
- **The Wendfolk's hearth patron,** beside Greenhand, their maker.

## Open questions
- What is it? A being that was always in the tapestry, or something that grew there?
- Does it answer prayers, and how?
- Guest-right, the shared table and the closed door to strangers were first sketched for a hearth god. Do they belong to Demerde?
