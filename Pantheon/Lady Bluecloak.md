# Lady Bluecloak

Neutral goddess of nature and animals. Also called Mielikki.

## What we know
- **Her realm holds much of some elements (decided 26 Oct).** She is not the definitive goddess of any element, but a lot of certain elements is contained in her tapestry. Which elements is still open. (Older text: "she contained elemental energies, which may be the origin of the realms of magic".)
- "Mielikki" is an existing Forgotten Realms goddess (9.9).
- **She and [[Tapio]] were once a married wizard couple (decided 31 Oct).** "Once": they are not together now. In Finnish myth, Mielikki is Tapio's wife. **Why they parted:** Tapio was fine with the mantle ritual; Lady Bluecloak was neutral, but opposed it at first. Now each is in their own domain, and they cannot meet as often as they would like.

## Connections
- [[The Martyr]]: another god who contained something dangerous (9.10).
