# The Current Era

The age after [[The Sundering]], the war that ended the Elden Era. The gods have ascended, the [[Levont]] has formed, and [[Moon Shards]] still fall from the debris belt.

## What we know
- **It began about 600 years ago** with the end of the Sundering (decided 22 Oct, see [[Timeline]]).
- Many war lords ascended as the good gods around its start ([[The Apotheosis]]).
- [[The Founding of the Levont]] and [[The Contest of the 41 Pillars]] happen in this age.
- Moon shards power magic and [[The Railroad]].

## Open questions
- What is it called in the world? Calendarium will show this name next to the year.
- What year is it now, and is the count from the Sundering, the Apotheosis or the Founding? (14.19)
