# Moon Shards

Small stones that fall from the debris belt of the shattered moon. They hold the **raw power of [[The Martyr]]**, who chose to die in the Moonshard Calamity and let her essence shatter to create the tapestries. The power is unaligned to any domain. It fuels magic and runs the trains, which makes the shards one of the pillars of the Levont's economy.

"Moon shards" is a working name, taken from the Moon Shard Storm. Rename it if the stones have their own name.

## What we know
- The world once had three moons. One broke, and two remain (29 Oct, [[The Moon's Shattering]]).
- Its debris formed a belt around the planet, like the rings of real planets.
- Pieces sometimes fall to the world as tiny rocks filled with power and energy.
- The stones hold fragments of the Martyr's unfinished mantle: she died before it fully materialised ([[Divine Essences]]). The power is raw and belongs to no domain.
- Scholars and theologians interpret the shards as **the Martyr's tears**. It is an interpretation, not a known fact.
- **Stable and clean on their own.** Shards do not thin [[The Veil]] by themselves. Some ways of using them may be dangerous.
- The stones can power magic.
- [[The Railroad]] runs on them.

## The graders (decided 25 Oct)
Shards are sorted by size and purity, like diamonds, and only big ones fit a furnace. Whoever grades them sets the price of everything shards touch, from a train ticket to a caster's fee.
- **The weak point:** small shards are nearly worthless. Anyone who finds a way to burn small shards would make the graders' scale meaningless overnight. A disruptive innovation, and a ready "why now".
- **The chain:** fall-chasers find them, graders sort them, carriers move them, and the railroad and casters burn them. Each link is a possible faction ([[Worldbuilding Sage Lessons#Economy and trade]]).

Still open: who the graders are (a guild? a council office? the Veingold?) and what the grades are called.

## Open questions
Sanderson's Second Law: limitations matter more than powers. These questions decide what the stones can't do.
- What are they called in the world?
- How often do they fall, and where? Predictably, or at random?
- Who owns a fallen shard: the finder, the landowner, the council, the Order? Is there a claims law?
- Do shards run out? What is left of a spent shard?
- Are they dangerous to find or carry? Using them is safe in ordinary ways, but some uses may be dangerous.
- Can anyone use one, or only trained people?
- Do they connect to [[The Tapestries]] or the veil (14.4)?
- Can shard power exceed the limits the gods put on [[Magic]]?
- **What does raw power do to [[The Veil]]?** (Answered) Nothing by itself: shards are stable and clean. Which uses are dangerous, and why?
- **Can enough shards be shaped into a new essence?** That would be a path to godhood outside the gods' limits, and a reason for the gods, the council and [[The Gods of Desirenthropy]] to care who collects them.
- The Martyr's church: is burning shards sacrilege, a blessing, or a gift she meant for the world?
- Does a user's own intent give the raw power a direction (fire, healing, beasts)?
