# The Sunturner

Good goddess of healing, ambition and the sun. She is a mentor, not a ruler: nonchalant, and unwilling to lead her own church. Her title comes from the heliotrope, the flower that keeps turning to face the sun.

## The title
**The Sunturner.** *Heliotrope* is Greek for "sun-turner". In Ovid's myth, the nymph Clytie watched the sun god for nine days without food or drink and became a heliotrope, a flower that keeps turning toward the sun.
- **Light:** ambition as a flower always turning toward the light. It pairs with her Order's motto, "The sun rises, and so do we."
- **Shadow:** in Clytie's story, devotion tips into obsession. A goddess of ambition carries that risk in her very name.
- She was formerly called "the Dawnflower", which is Sarenrae's title in Pathfinder ([[Borrowed Ideas]]).

## What we know
- **Domains (draft):** Healing, Ambition, Sun, Protection, Might. Five is too many, and Protection overlaps [[The Martyr]] (4.1).
- **Followers:** healers and midwives. The [[Order of the Rising Sun]] serves her.
- **The youngest of the gods (decided 8 Nov):** she was one of the newer humans, made not long before [[The Sundering]], and later one of the five makers of humans ([[Humans]]). In a sense this makes her the youngest of the gods.
- **Origin (decided 22 Oct):** before she ascended, she was a human wizard-lord of the old border kingdom. Its knights followed her into [[The Sundering]], and they founded the [[Order of the Rising Sun]].
- **Abilities:** has Rebuke Death, which may conflict with [[The Bud and the Sprout]] (9.18).
- **Appearance:** currently tall, golden-haired and blue-eyed.
- **Symbol (suggestion):** a heliotrope, or a flower head turned toward a sun.
- Helps the lost, which overlaps the [[Green Wardens]]' search and rescue (13.12).

## Light and shadow
She is the clearest gray god. She is a goddess of **Ambition** who helped close the path to godhood, so her faith tells mortals to strive while her own side made sure they can't go all the way. Her armed order is a rich armored aristocracy. If 4.1 cuts her domains to three, keeping Ambition keeps this tension.

## Her tapestry: the Dawning Vale (decided 31 Oct)
Like every tapestry, it embodies its essence rather than containing it: a realm where striving toward the light is everywhere and stronger ([[The Tapestries]]). It replaces the 26 Oct draft, the Long Noon. Other names considered: the First Light, the Morning Vale.
- **Rule:** everything turns toward the light and grows toward it. The sun is always rising and never quite up, so every moment is a beginning.
- **Image:** a glorious sunrise spilling over a mountain rim into a deep valley of flowers, every flower head turning to follow the light, like her heliotrope.
- **Who lives there:** larks and sun-birds, and the souls of her knights, forever climbing toward the rim. Wounds close in that light.

### Near its thin spots ([[The Veil]])
- **Hope and ambition rise:** people start ventures, apprentices challenge masters, the sick rally.
- **Monsters grow ambitious too:** packs range further, beasts hunt boldly by day, a horde chief starts dreaming of empire.
- **Flowers lean toward the thin spot,** not the sun, and mornings come early and linger.

### Her shadow
From Clytie's myth, where devotion tips into obsession: her mantle bends hope into the inability to give up. Near her thin spots, people chase a goal even when it will kill them.

## Other title candidates (not chosen)
- **The Day's Eye:** "daisy" comes from Old English *dæges eage*, because it opens at dawn and closes at dusk. Could serve as a regional or older title.
- **The Waking Lotus:** from the Egyptian god Nefertem, "Water-Lily of the Sun".
